### 1. Two definitions found in use across the campaign's own documents

| Def | Formula | Where used (found in raw logs / docs) |
|---|---|---|
| **def1** (reserved/effective-worker accounting) | `workers × wall_clock_hours / N_seeds` | The registered `0.969` anchor (`AMENDMENT_A1_VM1_NULL_AT_N100.md` §7: `00:58:08 × 15 cores / 15 seeds`); the abort-criterion (e) scoring in `SCAN_READOUT.md` (`S33 = 1.0984`) |
| **def3** (sacct-measured busy-CPU accounting) | `sacct "CPU Utilized" hours / N_seeds` | The `3.79 CPU-h/seed` venue-transfer anchor (`PREREGISTRATION_VENUE_TRANSFER.md` §11: `94.63 CPU-h / 25 seeds`, where 94.63 h is the sacct CPU-Utilized field, not workers×wall); one narrative sentence in `SCAN_READOUT.md` D-8 ("f_i=0 cells ran at 0.011–0.019 CPU-h/seed") |

def1 and def3 diverge by roughly `1/CPU-efficiency`. At high efficiency (~85–90%, the dosed
cells) they differ by only ~4–10%. At low efficiency (~33–48%, the zero-dose cells, which are
mostly idle waiting on a small non-parallelized fraction of the run) they diverge by up to ~3×.
This is the reconciliation the commission asked for.

### 4. A genuine internal inconsistency found, worth a request-change

`SCAN_READOUT.md` D-8 states in the same paragraph: "worst per-seed 1.0984 CPU-h (S33)... `f_i = 0`
cells ran at **0.011–0.019** CPU-h/seed." The first figure (S33, 1.0984) is def1. The second range
does **not** reproduce under def1 for the zero-dose cells (S00/S10/S20/S30 give def1 = 0.0281 /
0.0361 / 0.0375 / 0.0347 — roughly double the quoted range) but **does** reproduce almost exactly
under def3 (0.0101 / 0.0146 / 0.0190 / 0.0154, matching "0.011–0.019" to within rounding). So the
same sentence in the same document silently switches accounting convention mid-paragraph. This
does not affect any PASS/FAIL scoring (the abort criterion, which is the only place the convention
is load-bearing, stayed on def1 throughout, correctly), but it is a presentation defect: a reader
comparing the "0.969 anchor" against the "0.011–0.019" range would be comparing def1 to def3
without being told so. **Request-change: `SCAN_READOUT.md` D-8 should either (a) recompute the
`0.011–0.019` figure under def1 (giving ≈0.028–0.038), or (b) explicitly label both figures with
their accounting convention.**

### 5. The "~3.9× faster than 3.79" claim, checked under a consistent definition

Raw source for the `3.79` anchor: `results/venue_transfer_20260811/PREREGISTRATION_VENUE_TRANSFER.md`
§11, "task 28... wall 3:56:07, CPU 94.63 h / 25 seeds" → `94.63 / 25 = 3.79`. `94.63` is a sacct
CPU-Utilized-style figure (analogous to the "CPU Utilized" field seen throughout this campaign's
logs) — i.e. **`3.79` is a def3 number**, computed from a *different, earlier* campaign
(`venue_transfer_20260811`) whose raw SLURM epilogues are outside `mechanism_study_20260813/logs/`
and were not re-derivable bit-for-bit here (only the two summary numbers quoted in the prereg text
are available). `0.969` is a **def1** number (§2 above). The registered "`~3.9× faster`" claim
(`3.79 / 0.969 = 3.91`) therefore divides a def3 numerator by a def1 denominator.

Whether this matters depends on that task-28 job's own CPU efficiency, which can be back-derived
from the quoted figures: wall `3:56:07 = 3.9353 h`, and the job was a 25-seed `mp.Pool` batch (one
process per seed, so the *effective* worker count is 25, not the 64 cpus/task requested — the
prereg text itself makes this "single-process-per-seed" point). Effective-worker def1 for that job
= `25 × 3.9353 / 25 = 3.9353`. Efficiency = `94.63 / (25 × 3.9353) = 96.2%` — this job was
near-saturated, so def1 ≈ def3 for it (3.94 vs 3.79, an 3.8% gap), unlike the current campaign's
lightly-loaded zero-dose cells where def1/def3 diverge by up to 3×.

**Consistent-definition (def1/def1) ratio: `3.9353 / 0.9689 = 4.06`, i.e. "~4.1× faster," not
"~3.9× faster."** The registered claim is directionally correct and not materially wrong — both
numbers land in the same order of magnitude and support the same qualitative conclusion (a large,
real speedup plausibly from the Route 1 adaptive Gauss–Hermite contraction) — but the precise
multiplier quoted in the registered text (`~3.9×`) mixes def3 (numerator) with def1
(denominator); under either definition held consistent it comes out modestly higher (`~4.1×` def1,
or `94.63/25 ÷ 13.7553/15·... ` — recomputing fully in def3: MN0's def3 is `0.9170` §2, so
def3/def3 = `3.79 / 0.9170 = 4.13`, i.e. essentially the same conclusion, "~4.1× faster," under
**either** definition held consistent, both slightly above the registered "~3.9×"). **No band,
seed, or decision is affected — this is a wording-precision note, not a substantive correction —
but the exact multiplier should read "~4×" rather than "~3.9×" if re-quoted, and any future
cost-anchor comparison across campaigns should hold the definition (def1 or def3) fixed.**
