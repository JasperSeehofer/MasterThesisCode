# L4-T2 — numeric audit of the D1-D6 diff ledger

**Date:** 2026-08-15 · **Authorized:** ledger row #108 item 2 · **Status: PRESENTED, NOT
ADJUDICATED.** This document computes the numbers Part 1 (`L4_DER_PART1_20260815.md`, commit
`5e77e196`) registered as its own audit protocol (§4). It does not decide whether the D1-D6
account owns the residual — that call, and Part 2's closure, are the author's.

**Script:** `l4_t2_audit.py` · **Output:** `L4_T2_output.json` · ruff / ruff-format / mypy clean.

**Population:** the pinned 982-event population, loaded via
`darksiren_emri.validation.venue_transfer.build_venue_context` on the MN0X cell's committed
config (V-T3 pin-checked against the same CRB CSV / frozeng emit / pruned catalogue / injection
pool already recorded in `PINNED_INPUTS_MANIFEST.md`). All tilts are grid-neighbour central
differences at `h_true = 0.730` (`h = 0.725, 0.735`, Δh = 0.01) — the same geometry
`venue_transfer._slope_at_truth` / every prior L0/L4 script in this thread uses.

**A numerical-resolution finding surfaced along the way, reported for transparency:** a first
implementation of D2's z\*-search and D3's quadrature used the σ_z-kernel window alone (±5σ_z,
matching the production estimator's own window) to place quadrature/grid-search nodes. For a tail
of events with very tight CRB distance error (σ_d as small as 0.09%; worst case z_true=0.021,
implied GW-density peak width in z ≈ 1.9×10⁻⁵ — five orders of magnitude narrower than a typical
kernel window ~0.05–0.25), a fixed 50-node rule over the kernel-only window catastrophically
under-resolves the GW peak and produces pure node-placement noise (an early run gave D3(full dose)
≈ +18,600 nats/h, a sign that something was wrong, not a discovery). The fix: node placement uses
`min(kernel width, GW-peak width)` per event (`_effective_half_width`), which is provably correct
for the D3 comparison (both terms are centered at the same z near z_true when h ≈ h_true) and
converges cleanly (N_QUAD = 50/100/200 agree to 9 significant figures on the fixed version; see
§1 for the honest population-tail sensitivity that *survives* this fix, which is real, not
numerical).

**D1 verification.** The estimator's own tabulated α(h) reproduces the registered 1.036 exponent
to within 0.5% — a small, non-zero tension worth carrying forward but not large enough to move
D1's headline value materially (1,400.6 vs 1,393.6, a 0.5% shift).

**D2's z\*-tracking correction is large and real.** Allowing the integrand peak to move with h
(rather than assuming it pinned at z_true) removes **~90% of the naive +N/h magnitude**
(−134 vs −1,345), exactly the "D″D/D′²-class term" the audit protocol named as the subtle piece.
The with/without-p_pop ambiguity is not decision-relevant (1.2% apart); both are reported per the
instruction never to pick silently.

**D3's exact-z-limit value is not analytically negligible, despite the leading-order argument.**
A first-order expansion (correct-minus-coded exponent diff ~ O((1−u)³), u=h_true/h) predicts the
population tilt should vanish to leading order near h_true — and it does, **per event, for
typical σ_d** — but the diff scales as ε³/σ_d², and a tail of exceptionally well-measured (tiny
σ_d) events breaks the "small" assumption: the top 10 events (1% of the population, all with
σ_d < 0.003) contribute **−300 of the total −541** (55%); excluding them leaves −242, still not
negligible. This is a genuine population-tail effect, not a numerical artifact (verified stable
under 4× finer central-difference and grid resolution) — but it means D3's "O(σ_d) asymmetric"
characterization in Part 1 undersells its population-level size once the σ_d tail is included.

D4 needs a different (non-quadratic) account, D3's population-tail contribution needs further
characterization, or a term outside D1-D6 is missing from the ledger** — a finding for Part 2, not

## 3. Dose-structure test (kill test, §4 item 3)

Predicted T_res(f) = T_D3(f) [this audit] + D4(f) [restated, already-refuted M1 account];
tolerance = 3× the level SEs = 150 nats/h, as specified.

| dose f | T_D3 (this audit) | D4 (restated) | T_res predicted | T_res measured | diff | within tolerance? |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 0.25 | −734.75 | −111 | −845.75 | +699 | −1,544.75 | **NO** |
| 0.50 | −657.50 | −384 | −1,041.50 | +149 | −1,190.50 | **NO** |
| 1.00 | −342.44 | −990 | −1,332.44 | −62 | −1,270.44 | **NO** |

**Verdict: FAILS at all three dose levels, by roughly an order of magnitude relative to the
150-nat tolerance at every level.** D3+D4 do not reproduce the measured T_res(f) dose curve. Two
structural observations, reported without adjudication:

- The measured T_res(f) **decreases monotonically** with dose (+699 → +149 → −62); the predicted
  T_D3(f) also decreases in magnitude toward full dose (−735 → −658 → −342) but stays
  **negative throughout**, while the measured curve **changes sign** (positive at low/mid dose,
  slightly negative at full dose). D3 alone therefore cannot be "the" dose-decay mechanism the
  kill test was checking for — its sign is wrong at two of the three levels even before adding
  D4.
- Confirms, independently of the M6R verifier's own already-registered conclusion, that **D1 and
  D2 being dose-blind (as designed) is not itself informative about T_res's dose structure**,
  since D3 (which IS dose-dependent) fails to reproduce it either.

## 4. 2D channel — rough estimate (§4 item 4)

Method: per-event T_D3 at full dose, reweighted by 1/σ_Mz (a crude proxy for where the 2D mass
channel's g_i factor is informative — NOT a recomputation of the actual `completion_mass_factor_g`
machinery, which item 4 explicitly does not require), normalized to preserve the event count, vs
the plain unweighted sum. The weighted-minus-unweighted excess is converted to bias units via
`/Ā` (AJREN's own curvature, same displacement-law geometry as §2/L4-T1).

| quantity | value |
|---|---:|
| T_D3 unweighted, full dose | −342.44 nats/h |
| T_D3 mass-precision-weighted, full dose | −342.05 nats/h |
| excess (weighted − unweighted) | +0.386 nats/h |
| excess in bias units (via Ā, 1D) | **+1.35×10⁻⁵** |
| excess in bias units (via Ā, 2D) | +1.35×10⁻⁵ |
| Stage-3 2D-only sub-additivity reference | +0.0027 |
| order-of-magnitude match? | **NO — ~200× too small** |

**This rough proxy finds essentially no D3-sourced 2D-only excess** — the mass-precision-weighted
and unweighted T_D3 differ by well under 1 nats/h, converting to a bias-unit excess ~200× smaller
than the observed Stage-3 sub-additivity. This is directionally consistent with L4-T1 §4's own
finding that the 2D-vs-1D excess "looks like a property carried by the estimator's tilt broadly
... rather than something introduced newly by [a specific term]" — this crude D3-only proxy adds a
second, independent data point in the same direction, without being able to rule the mechanism in
or out definitively (the g_i proxy here is explicitly rough, per the task's own allowance).

## 5. Summary

- D1 restated and numerically verified (exponent within 0.5%).
- D2's naive +N/h is confirmed analytically in the exact-z limit, but the GLADE-mix z\*-tracking
  correction is large (removes ~90% of the naive magnitude) — the audit's most informative
  new numeric result, in either p_pop reading (they agree to 1.2%).
- D3 computed for the first time (Part 1 left it "TBD numerically"): small at exact-z **on
  average** but not negligible once a tail of exceptionally well-measured events is included;
  substantial and dose-decaying (in magnitude) in the GLADE-mix reading, but wrong-signed against
  the measured T_res dose curve at two of three levels.
- D4 restated verbatim (already refuted elsewhere) and D5 restated + converted to nats/h (small).
- **The full ledger does NOT close against T(MN0X), T(AM2P), or T(AJREN) under any reading** —
  every predicted value is negative where every measured value is strongly positive, by 70–210σ.
- **The dose-structure kill test FAILS at all three dose levels.**
- **The 2D-channel rough estimate finds no material D3-sourced excess** (~200× too small).

None of this is adjudicated here. It is the numeric input Part 1 called for before any mechanism
claim is made; the author and Part 2 decide what it means — including whether D4 needs replacing,
whether D3's tail-dominated character changes its role, or whether a term outside D1-D6 is
required.
