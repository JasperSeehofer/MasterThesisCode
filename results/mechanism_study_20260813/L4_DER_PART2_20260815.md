## 1. Why the Part-1 ledger could not close (leads (a) and (b), resolved)

The audit composed predicted tilts as **sums of isolated per-term tilts** of (coded − correct)
diffs, anchored on "correct-form tilt ≡ 0". Both moves fail here, for reasons now derivable:

1. **(lead a) Isolated-term tilts do not add inside a log of an integral.** Every D-term lives
   inside `ln Σ_k ∫ dz kern · p_gw · (…)`; the tilt of the log-sum is the *responsibility-weighted*
   sum of integrand log-derivatives, and each term's h-derivative interacts with the moving
   integrand peak. Pricing D2 "along the tracked peak" while pricing D3 "with everything else held
   fixed" mixes incompatible frames; their sum is not the tilt of anything.
2. **(lead b) "Correct-form tilt ≡ 0" is true only on the correct form's own generative measure —
   and the cancellation that makes it true is exactly the piece the coded form lacks.** The
   correct d_obs-density (Part 1 §2) *also* contains the positive mass-growth term derived in §2
   below; its expected score vanishes because the p_pop-weighted, correctly normalized density
   cancels that growth in expectation over the population (the negative share concentrates in the
   population roll-off / exponent-expectation structure). The coded estimator replaces p_pop with
   per-candidate kernels and a flat 1/K — it keeps the positive term and discards its cancellation
   partner. Transferring "correct = 0" onto the coded ledger therefore silently deleted the
   dominant positive entry.

## 2. The derivation — the convolution frame and the mass-growth term

**Regime.** The coded GW factor `norm.pdf(d_L(z,h)/d_obs; 1, σ_d)`, as a function of z, is a
Gaussian of z-width σ_gw = σ_d·D(z\*)/D′(z\*) centred at the peak z\*(h) where D(z\*) = h·d_obs
(D(z) ≡ h·d_L(z,h), h-independent). On the pinned population σ_gw has median **0.0125**
(p5–p95: 0.0005–0.026), while the GLADE σ_z mix runs ~0.01–0.25: for the typical candidate the
GW factor is the **narrow** one and is *integrated over* by the kernel quadrature — the estimator
operates in the photo-z-starved regime, not the "GW likelihood evaluated at a data point" regime
in which Part 1's D2/D3 pricing was framed.

**Per-candidate closed form.** Linearizing d_L about z\* and completing the square,

    c1_k(h) ≈ (σ_gw/σ_d) · N(z_obs,k ; z*(h), √(σ_k² + σ_gw²))        (window clipping aside)

**Score decomposition.** With L_e = (1/K)Σ_k c1_k and responsibilities r_k = c1_k/Σc1_k, the
per-event tilt splits into

    d ln L_e/dh =  G_e                                    ← mass growth (this note's object)
                +  Σ_k r_k (z_obs,k − z*)/(σ_k²+σ_gw²) · dz*/dh        ← ball-density drift
                +  width/quadratic terms (cancel in expectation)
                +  window-motion boundary flux (M7-class)

with

    G_e = d ln σ_gw(z*(h))/dh = (1/h)·(1 − x_e),   x_e = D·D″/D′² at z*_e.

**The mass-growth term G_e is the missing positive source.** It is the h-growth of the z-space
*volume* of the ratio-form GW factor: raising h moves z\* up and widens the factor's z-footprint
(σ_gw = σ_d·h·d_obs/D′), so every event's integrated likelihood rises with h — no D1–D6 row prices
it. Population sum (pinned z_true, no noise):

    Σ_e G_e = N/h − Σ_e x_e/h = +1345.2 − 285.7 = **+1059.5 nats/h**.

**The identity that retro-explains A-M2′.** The installed Jacobian repair multiplies the integrand
by dd_L/dz/d_obs, turning the GW factor into a z-density with **h-independent unit mass** — it
removes exactly Σ G_e (plus per-node reshaping). And indeed the M6R verifier's J prediction was
"−N/h + D′-tracking (+291)" = −1054: **term for term the negative of Σ G_e = −(N/h − Σx/h) =
−1059.5** (the "+291 tracking piece" *is* Σx/h = +285.7 up to its own approximation). This is why
A-M2′ measured on-prediction (−1132.9 ± 35.9) while the base estimator's positive tilt stayed
unexplained: the same object was priced as a *repair effect* but never entered the base ledger as
a *base-estimator term*.

**Lead (c) resolved (in the negative for 1/K):** the flat 1/K normalization is h-independent and
carries no tilt; the missing term is the mass growth, not the host-weight substitution. **Lead (d)
rescoped:** D3 (exponent scale) acts inside the same integral and must be priced with the same
responsibility weighting — done exactly by the `exp` switch below.
