## 1. The generator, as the code actually is

Per event i, all conditioning on the pinned rows (982 events; **no detection draw anywhere**):

1. (z_true,i, d_true,i, σ_dL,i) pinned (`_draw_seed_realization`, VT-D1).
2. **d_obs,i = d_true,i·(1 + σ_dL,i·e1)**, e1 ~ N(0,1) — a Gaussian density in d_obs with mean
   d_true and width σ_dL·d_true (fractional noise about the TRUE distance).
3. Ball (`draw_ball_pinned`): window [d_obs(1−4σ), d_obs(1+4σ)] mapped to z through the FIXED
   catalog table (`imp_dl_nodes/imp_z_nodes` — no h anywhere); **K_i − 1 impostors iid from the
   catalog density restricted to that window**; host inserted; order shuffled.
4. σ_z texture: per-candidate σ_k from the GLADE z-decile sampler; **z_obs,k = z_k + f·σ_k·ε_k.**

Data visible to the estimator: d_obs,i and {z_obs,k, σ_k}, K_i (plus M_z_obs for 2D).

## 2. The correct venue likelihood, derived

With pinned events, the h-dependent physics enters ONLY through step 2 evaluated at the host.
Marginalizing the unknown host identity and host redshift:

p(data_i | h) ∝ Σ_k P(k = host) · ∫ dz p_pop(z) N(z_obs,k; z, σ_k) ·
  **N(d_obs,i ; D(z)/h , σ_dL,i·D(z)/h)** · [impostor factors]

Three structural facts fall out immediately:

- **(F1) The impostor factors are h-independent.** Impostors are drawn from the catalog density in
  a window set by d_obs (data) through a fixed table — given d_obs they carry no h. They normalize
  out of the h-posterior entirely. Corollary: the coded per-candidate window truncation and its
  renormalization are ball-*construction* devices, not likelihood terms — consistent with M3 and
  the REN arm both measuring small effects.
- **(F2) There is no α term.** −N ln α models detection selection; the venue generator never
  selects. In production α is cancelled in expectation by the detection factor in the data
  density; **with pinned events nothing cancels it — the coded α term is a pure spurious tilt of
  +1.036·N/h = +1393.6 nats/h.** (M4's finding — α's σ_z-blindness is internally correct — stands;
  the new statement is about α's *presence in this venue*, which M4 did not examine.)
- **(F3) The GW term is a density in d_obs, not a ratio-likelihood.** The coded
  `norm.pdf(d_L/d_obs; 1, σ_d)` differs from the correct N(d_obs; μ, σ_d·μ), μ = D(z)/h, in TWO
  ways: the **normalization prefactor 1/(σ_d·μ)** — whose h-part is **+ln h per event, tilt
  +N/h = +1345.2 nats/h, missing from the code** — and the **exponent's scale**: correct
  −(d_obs−μ)²/(2σ_d²μ²) vs coded −(μ−d_obs)²/(2σ_d²d_obs²) — equal at leading order, differing by
  an O(σ_d) asymmetric term with its own h-structure. Note also: **in this form no |d d_L/dz|
  Jacobian appears** (the z-integral runs against z-densities; the d_obs-density needs no change
  of variables) — so the A-M2′ "J-restoration" was itself a *proxy* whose −ln h piece imitates
  part of the correct form's structure rather than being a term of it.
