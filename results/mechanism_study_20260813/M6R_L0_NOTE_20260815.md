# M6-revision L0 — tilt decomposition: alpha + measured J + dose-dependent residual

**Status: PRESENTED, NOT ADJUDICATED.** This note proposes a revised M6 candidate (M6′) and three
kill tests for it. No branch call, repair, or register status change is made here; that is the
author's. Ledger row #103 (author-authorized).

**Context.** `M6_L0_KILLTESTS_20260814.md` killed M6-as-registered (pure σ_z-blind tilt ×
dose-controlled curvature, with the tilt asserted **dose-independent**) on tests (i) tilt
dose-invariance and (iii) α-share, under the registered ("all `f_h > 0` cells") operationalization
— both KILL. Test (ii) bias/σ²_post constancy SURVIVEd. The kill-test note's own comparison block
noted that the composite's headline numbers are reproduced almost exactly on the narrower
**fixed-`f_i = 1.0` column** `{S13, S23, S33}` — i.e. M6 survives at full impostor dose. This note
characterizes *why*: the tilt itself is dose-dependent, and the dependence is carried by `f_i`
(impostor dose), not `f_h` (host dose). It also measures the missing-J piece **directly** for the
first time (A-M2′ ran since the kill-test note was written) rather than relying on the closed-form
prediction alone.

**Data:** the 16 `S00`–`S33` dose-grid cells, `MN0X` (N = 100, base estimator, full dose),
`AM2P_h0p730_results_seeds0_25.json` (A-M2′, J restored, full dose, N = 25, fresh seeds
+53000…+53024), `ANULL_h0p730_results_seeds0_15.json` (A-NULL, ×1.7 constant, full dose, N = 15,
seed-paired with `MN0X`'s first 15). All quantities recomputed from raw per-seed `ln_post_1d` /
`ln_post_2d` vectors and per-seed `map_*`/`post_sd_*` fields — the per-file `aggregate` block is
never read (`sum_dlog_gfrac_dh` there is the Σ ln(L2/L1) gfrac slope, a different quantity).

Script: `m6r_l0_decomposition.py` (this directory). Output: `M6R_L0_output.json`.

## Method

- **Slope at truth T.** Central difference of `ln_post` on the two `h_grid` neighbours of
  `h_true = 0.730` (`h = 0.725`, `h = 0.735`, Δh = 0.01) — matches
  `venue_transfer._slope_at_truth` and `m6_l0_killtests.py` verbatim. Seed-averaged per cell/arm,
  both channels, with SE = sd/√N.
- **Degenerate `f_h = 0` cells (S00–S03).** The host is exact under `f_h = 0`, so in ≥ 59/60 seeds
  the posterior collapses onto a single grid point (`M6_L0_KILLTESTS`/`SCAN_READOUT` §1.7:
  bias, per-seed sd, and `post_sd` all exactly 0.0 for these four cells). A central difference on
  a near-delta log-posterior measures local curvature/floor artifacts, not a smooth linear tilt —
  the measured `T` for these cells swings **from −36,532 to +53,208 nats/h** with SEs of
  19,000–39,000 nats/h (below), i.e. it is pure noise at this resolution. **T is reported for
  S00–S03 for completeness but excluded from every fit and from the "reliable" cell groups.**
- **`f_i = 0` column (S10/S20/S30).** Not degenerate (σ_post ≠ 0), but close to it: these three
  cells have the smallest `post_sd` of the 12 `f_h > 0` cells (host-only informative, impostors
  exact). §4 below shows this column also breaks the tilt×curvature closure (ratios 3–215×), for
  the same reason — flagged, not silently included in the "interior" fits.

**Interior-9 (f_h > 0, f_i > 0) ratio statistics:** 1D mean 0.749 ± 0.046 (sd), range
[0.656, 0.796]; 2D mean 0.748 ± 0.040, range [0.658, 0.779]. Despite T itself spanning a **34%**
range across these 9 cells (2,642.9–3,544.3 nats/h, 1D), the ratio measured-bias/(T·σ²_post) is
stable within roughly ±6% of its mean, both channels.

**This directly reconciles test (i)'s KILL with test (ii)'s SURVIVE.** Test (i) fails because T
varies substantially with dose (up to 38% deviation from the grand mean on the registered 12-cell
set). Test (ii) survives because σ²_post *co-varies* with dose in the opposite sense (large at high
combined dose, tiny at low dose), and multiplying the cell's *own* dose-dependent T by its *own*
σ²_post — not a fixed constant times σ²_post — yields a product that tracks bias with an
approximately fixed proportionality (~0.75, both channels) across the interior-9 set, even though
neither factor alone is dose-invariant. Test (ii)'s bias/σ²_post ratio (§ `M6_L0_KILLTESTS`, factor
1.38–1.41 across the same 9 cells) is thus not an independent constancy — it is largely the T
variation folded back in, evaluated as a ratio rather than reported as T itself.

The ~0.75 proportionality (i.e. bias ≈ 0.75 × T·σ²_post, or equivalently T·σ²_post overshoots bias
by ~1/0.75 ≈ 1.33×) sits close to the prereg's own disclosed local-Gaussian approximation scale
error: "2739 × 1.924e-5 = 0.0527 vs measured +0.0373" → ratio 0.0373/0.0527 = 0.708 — the same
effect, independently reproduced here across 9 cells rather than one comparison point.

**The f_i = 0 column (S10/S20/S30) breaks this closure badly** (ratios 3.05–214.7): σ_post is tiny
there (near-degenerate, host-only informative posterior), so the denominator T·σ²_post collapses
toward zero while the numerator (bias) does not — the same near-delta-posterior regime that
excludes S00–S03 from § 3's fits also invalidates the local-Gaussian approximation underlying this
closure at f_i = 0. This is disclosed, not swept in with the interior-9 mean.

## 5. Proposed M6′ (revised candidate statement)

**M6-as-registered** (σ_z-blind tilt, dose-**independent**, × dose-controlled curvature) is refuted
by test (i)/(iii) on the registered operationalization. The data above show *why* and *what
survives*: the tilt is real, large, and does approximately govern the bias through the same
curvature-controlled multiplicative structure — but the tilt itself has **three** components, only
one of which (α) is truly dose-independent.

> **M6′ (proposed).** The aggregate log-posterior tilt at truth decomposes as
> `T(f_h, f_i) ≈ T_α + T_J(f_i) + T_res(f_i)`, where:
> - `T_α = +1,393.6` nats/h (analytic, σ_z-blind, dose-independent — unchanged from M6).
> - `T_J(f_i)` is the missing-Jacobian tilt, measured directly at full dose as
>   `−1,132.9 ± 36.0` nats/h (84% of the −1,345.2 nats/h closed-form prediction; sign and order
>   confirmed, magnitude ~16% below prediction) and inferred (not yet directly measured at partial
>   dose — A-M2′ ran only at full dose) to **scale with f_i**, approaching its full-dose value only
>   as f_i → 1 (§3's R−1345.2 trend is consistent with `T_J(f_i)` itself being the f_i-dependent
>   piece, since T_α is fixed and the residual is defined relative to the FULL-dose J prediction).
> - `T_res(f_i)` is an additional, currently **unlocated** dose-dependent residual, largest at low
>   f_i (~1,700–1,900 nats/h at f_i ≈ 0–0.25 relative to the full-dose-J baseline) and decaying
>   toward ≈0 by f_i ≈ 1.0 (§3), roughly independent of f_h.
> - The MAP bias is well-described, on the interior-9 cells only, by
>   `bias ≈ 0.75 × T(f_h,f_i) · σ²_post(f_h,f_i)` (§4) — the curvature-controlled multiplicative
>   structure of M6 survives, with T now understood as itself dose-dependent rather than constant,
>   and a fixed ~0.75 proportionality absorbing the local-Gaussian approximation's known scale
>   error.
> - The closure and the tilt decomposition both **fail outside the interior-9 dose range**
>   (f_h = 0 or f_i = 0), where the posterior is too sharply peaked (near-delta or
>   near-degenerate) for either the central-difference tilt or the local-Gaussian curvature
>   approximation to be meaningful — this is a stated domain restriction of M6′, not a silent
>   exclusion.

**Where T_res could live (flagged, not asserted).** One candidate: window-edge/straddler effects —
candidates whose ball-window inclusion is decided on true z but whose kernel is evaluated at
scattered z_obs (the intake dossier's parity text, formalized as M7 in
`PREREGISTRATION_M2PRIME_ABLATION.md` §1: "host/impostor ball-window inclusion asymmetry").
**The possible identity `T_res ≡ M7` is flagged, not asserted, and the observed sign pattern is in
tension with the naive M7 direction:** M7's mechanism only applies to *dosed* (kernel-branch)
candidates (an undosed candidate has z_obs = z_true exactly, so there is no window-membership
mismatch to produce), which naively predicts an M7 contribution that *grows* with dose — the
opposite of the *decaying*-with-f_i trend measured for T_res here. Reconciling this sign tension
(or finding a different residual location whose natural direction is decay-with-dose) is exactly
the open question M6′'s kill tests below are built to probe, not something resolved by this note.

## 6. Three registered two-sided kill tests for M6′ (proposed; bands from committed data only)

**KT-M6′-1 (J-tilt sign and order, at full dose).** Prediction: `T(AM2P) − T(MN0X)` has the
predicted sign (negative) and falls within a factor **[0.5, 1.5]** of the closed-form −1,345.2
nats/h prediction, both channels. **Measured: −1,132.9/−1,133.0 nats/h (1D/2D), ratio 0.842 —
PASSES as measured** (this is a post-hoc report of what was found, not a fresh test; it is
registered here as the retained/re-usable form of the check for any future re-run, e.g. at
finer dose grades of A-M2′). Band derivation: [0.5, 1.5]× spans the demonstrated ~1.5× scale
error already disclosed by the prereg for the closely related local-Gaussian bias prediction —
reused here rather than invented, since no independent calibration of A-M2′'s own systematic
exists yet. **False-fail rate:** with SE = 36.0 nats/h on the measured value and a band edge at
±50% of 1345.2 (≈672.6 nats/h from the point prediction), the band is **≈18.7σ** wide at either
edge relative to the measured value's own SE — false-fail ≈ 0 at this N; the test is sensitive to
systematic (not statistical) model error, which is its purpose.

**KT-M6′-2 (residual monotonicity in f_i, interior-9).** Prediction: `R(f_h,f_i) − 1345.2`,
averaged over f_h at each of the three f_i levels {0.25, 0.50, 1.00}, is **monotonically
non-increasing** in f_i (two-sided: fails if the ordering is violated at any adjacent pair, in
**both** channels). Measured order (1D): 699.4 → 149.2 → −62.3 (strictly decreasing);
(2D): 826.3 → 274.1 → 64.0 (strictly decreasing) — **PASSES as measured.**
**False-fail rate:** the three f_i-level averages pool 3 cells each; using the per-cell SEs
(≈80–420 nats/h, §1) propagated as `σ/√3` per level, the level-to-level gaps (≈550, ≈210 nats/h)
clear 3× the propagated SE at both steps in 1D (propagated SE per level ≈ 50–90 nats/h) — false-fail
from measurement noise alone is small (≲ few %) but not independently quantified against a null
(no permutation/toy is used, per the toy-calibration bar); this is disclosed as a known gap, not
closed here.

**KT-M6′-3 (curvature-closure proportionality stability, interior-9).** Prediction: the ratio
`bias / (T · σ²_post)`, computed per interior-9 cell, has **max/min ≤ 1.5** across the 9 cells, in
both channels (a tighter, purpose-built version of the registered M6 test (ii), now applied to the
proportionality constant of M6′'s explicit bias law rather than to bias/σ²_post alone).
Measured: 1D max/min = 0.796/0.656 = **1.213**; 2D max/min = 0.779/0.658 = **1.184** —
**PASSES as measured**, with margin (1.21–1.24× against a 1.5× band, i.e. using roughly
40–55% of the allowed headroom). **False-fail rate:** derived from the observed sd/mean ≈ 6% (1D)
and 5.3% (2D) of the ratio across 9 cells — a 1.5× band is ≈8σ of that empirical scatter, so
false-fail from sampling noise at this N is small, though (as with KT-M6′-2) no toy-independent
null distribution is available to state a calibrated false-fail probability; reported as a
data-derived bound, not a toy-calibrated rate.

**Caveat common to all three:** these are *reports of what the committed data already show*, run
once, not blind pre-registered predictions tested against unseen data — the task explicitly asked
for kill tests to accompany the M6′ proposal, and this is the honest limit of what "kill tests
registered from data already examined to build the candidate" can mean without new arms. Any
future re-run (e.g. A-M2′ at partial dose, to measure `T_J(f_i)` directly rather than infer it from
the R-decomposition) would be a genuine blind test of KT-M6′-1/2 as stated; as recorded here they
are consistency checks on the same dataset used to derive M6′, not independent confirmations.
