## Verdict

**DEFECT-HARDENED is not earned.** The test was not vacuous in implementation — arm A really does integrate a different domain with a different rule, and I confirm the aligned `B_num` differs from production's by the expected +6.3e-5 tail mass — but it was vacuous in **power**: because the ±4σ window is defined in `d_L`, its truncation is h-invariant to first order, so aligning domain and quadrature can move the statistic by at most ~1e-5 against a band half-width of 0.0582. DEFECT-HARDENED was arithmetically guaranteed before any seed ran, and the one arm component that had leverage — the S̄_φ factor, which the prereg's arm A explicitly registered ("outside `[1e-6, z_max(h)]` the selection factor is exactly 0") — was removed from the primary arm post-registration without re-deriving the bands. Restoring it, on the same 15 seeds with everything else identical, moves S̄₁₅ from **−0.117321** to **+0.007604 ± 0.018361** (0.41σ from zero, inside the PAIRING-OWNS-IT band), a shift of **+0.124925 ± 0.001194 = 106.5%** of the banked score. The registered A19 falsifier for DEFECT-HARDENED therefore fires. **The most likely — and, at the measured resolution, the sufficient — locus is item 1 of the (e) checklist: the completion numerator omits the S̄_φ survival factor that its own normalizer `β_Ḡ_φ` carries and that the generator applied at accept time (`S_4D`, `selfgen_control.py:575-583`).** That is the legacy `selection_in_completion_numerator="off"` cell — labelled "not a production posterior" by the estimator's own log line in every C-SG run, and already superseded in-tree by the `"fused"` cell from rows #117–#118. What is bankable is the 6σ non-zero score *as a demonstrated numerator/normalizer mismatch in the `off` cell*, now with its mechanism identified; what should be withdrawn is the inference that the mismatch is upstream of implementation convention, and the INTERNAL-DEFECT label in its "deeper estimator math" reading. The next measurement is not another quadrature refinement: it is re-running one C-SG-F seed under `"fused"` end-to-end (both legs, not just my numerator patch) and checking that the matched score is null there.
