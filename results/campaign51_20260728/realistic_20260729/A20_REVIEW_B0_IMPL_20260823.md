**FINDING 1 (item 1) — kernel family: the generator does NOT use the estimator's numerator kernel. VERDICT: FATAL.**
The b0i runs execute under `PRODUCTION_FLAGS` (`--normalization_mode absolute_marginal`, `--host_z_kernel volume_deconv`, correspondence_1d.py:319-328), and in that cell the estimator's per-host numerator z-kernel is `galaxy_redshift_prior_pdf(z) = N(z; z_g, σ_eff)·w_pop(z)·f_k(z)/Z_g` with `w_pop = dVc/dz/(1+z)` and the C7-core host-pixel completeness factor (bayesian_statistics.py:5954-5961 — "absolute_marginal … keeps the volume_deconv kernel"; :5990-6023). The new `kernel_smeared_survival`/`_draw_kernel_survival_redshifts` (correspondence_1d.py:~1095/1230) and the driver's `mass_companion` Σ̃^φ use the **bare Gaussian**, self-labeled "a registered simplification" — but PA-2 registers no such simplification, and the A20 review's own alignment logic (Finding 2: p_gen must equal the estimator's q_G; it rejected the importance-weight fallback precisely for leaving "an unquantified O(1)-class venue term against exactly the B-T PASS branch") requires the estimator's own kernel. Measured on 200k real catalogue rows: the z_true draw density is mis-centered by **median +0.32σ_g (p95 +0.70σ_g; Δz median +0.0126)** and per-host S̃ is off by ~3% (S̃_deconv/S̃_bare = 0.9703, p5-p95 [0.965, 0.979]; w-weighted Σ̃ ratio 0.9712). That is a first-order venue term against exactly B-T's I=0 PASS target — the false-verdict machine PA-2 exists to prevent. Note also the inverted-inference point: "the catalogue's declared error model" is p(z_obs|z_true); the induced z_true|z_obs draw is the volume-deconvolved kernel — the bare form is the inversion error the production volume_deconv fix corrects.
**Fix:** evaluate k_g as `N(z; z_g, σ_eff)·w_pop(z)·f_k(z at the host's pixel, ZoA fallback per :5986-5988)`, renormalized on the ±4σ/1e-6-floored window (the Z_g convention), at h=H_TRUE, in `kernel_smeared_survival`, `_draw_kernel_survival_redshifts`, and the driver's `mass_companion` Σ̃^φ (the completeness object is already returned by `build_bsel_selection_objects` and threaded-but-unused). Alternatively an A21 amendment registering the bare-kernel simplification with a quantified bound — the ~3%/0.33σ numbers above suggest it cannot be waived.
