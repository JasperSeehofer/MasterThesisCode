### 1.3 What the correct object is NOT [INFER]

- **Not point-evaluated S_4D(z_g, M_g):** ignores both the z-kernel and the mass posterior;
  wrong whenever σ_z or σ_M is finite (production σ_M ~ 60–200%).
- **Not S̄_φ(z) per candidate:** S̄_φ marginalizes the survival over the POPULATION mass
  density φ (`:1961-1962`) — the completion class's object. A catalogue candidate's latent
  mass is distributed as its own posterior p_gal, not as φ; using S̄_φ would misweight the
  survival by the mass-prior mismatch (order the φ-vs-p_gal difference of S_4D means, NOT
  small at σ_M ≲ 1 dex).
- **Not S̃_φ,g (or a p_gal-smeared scalar) as a multiplicative constant:** any z-constant
  factor cancels between numerator and its event-level normalization only if it is also in
  the divisor chain; more importantly it drops the z- and data-coupling of the survival,
  which is the entire 2D channel (μ_cond moves with u(z), so mz_sel's S-weight is
  data-dependent).
