**FINDING 6 (item D) — heavy tails, trim, dead rows.** VERDICT: **REFUTED as registered (the 5%-trim as a verdict-participating twin), replacement below.**
Derivation of the pathology: Var of q_Ḡ/q_G under q_G-draws is ∫q_Ḡ²/q_G − 1, divergent wherever q_Ḡ's tails are heavier than q_G² (generator draws landing where the ball is starved or empty; the empty-ball case L_cat = 0 ⇒ w = 0 is an *infinite* summand, and restricting to "live rows" silently conditions it away, biasing the identity low by exactly the support-violation mass — Finding 1(a)). A 5% symmetric-purpose trim of a positive, mean-1-targeted, right-skewed ratio strictly biases the mean down, so a *correct* arm generically shows Ī_trim < Ī with band disagreement ⇒ falsifier (i) auto-UNDETERMINEDs the correct arm. Recommendations, in registered order:
(1) Primary unchanged in form — untrimmed per-seed mean, fleet SEM across the 12 seeds (seed means are iid; the CLT operates at seed level) — but with **dead-row accounting registered**: per arm, bank n_dead(a) = rows with L_cat_no_bh = 0; any dead row under a catalogue-hosted generator is a support violation (formally infinite contribution) and is reported as such, never dropped; a materially arm-dependent dead-row rate (registered: |Δrate| > 1/200) voids the arm comparison.
(2) Replace the 5%-trim twin with **PSIS** (Pareto-smoothed importance sampling, Vehtari et al.): fit a generalized Pareto to the top tail of (1−w)/w per arm, report k̂; the robustness twin is the PSIS-smoothed mean; UNDETERMINED(a) iff k̂ > 0.7 **and** raw/PSIS disagree in band. This grounds "heavy-tail dominance" in the standard diagnostic instead of an arbitrary trim fraction.
(3) **Per-seed median: refuted as a primary** — the identity constrains the mean only; the median of a skewed ratio targets a different functional and would fail a correct arm.
(4) **Reciprocal form: refuted for this venue** — E_{d~q_Ḡ}[w/(1−w)]·M_Ḡ/M_G = 1 needs Ḡ-class draws, which the class-conditioned b0 venue never produces.
(5) Add a **zero-variance mass companion**: the mass half of the identity (M_G^true per arm vs C\*) is a catalogue-sum computation (Σ_w, Σ^φ, Σ̃^φ from banked tables + kernel quadrature) — bank it as a registered secondary at machine precision; the Monte-Carlo mean then carries only the shape question.
