## 2. Method

Per seed, per arm (bt = twin, bc = coded):

```
A  = Σ_{e in F0-accepted, LIVE} (1 - w2_e)                    [reproduces the frozen LHS2]
C  = Σ_{e in F0-accepted, LIVE} (1 - w2_e) / S̄_φ(z_e)
B  = Σ_{e in all 200 drawn latents} 1 / S̄_φ(z_e)

LHS2_original_seed  = (C2*/200) · A
LHS2_corrected_seed = C2* · C / B
```

`w2_e = α_G_φ·L_cat_with_bh / (α_G_φ·L_cat_with_bh + B_num_wbh)`, LIVE = `L_cat_with_bh > 0`
(`event_likelihoods.csv`, h=0.73 rows only). F0 = `{σ_dL/d̂ < 0.10 ∧ SNR ≥ 20}`, applied to
`prepared_cramer_rao_bounds.csv`; the `event_idx`↔prepared-row mapping is 0-based (verified per
seed by intersecting the F0 mask with `event_idx`, not assumed).

**Data-quality note (not a mapping bug):** seed 900115 (both bt and bc) is missing exactly one
F0-passing row (prep row 82, SNR=258, rel-σ=0.025, comfortably inside F0) from `event_likelihoods.csv`
entirely — 94 F0-accepted by the prepared-CSV criteria vs 93 present in diagnostics, identically in
both arms. This is a pre-existing gap in the banked fleet (consistent with the row-#204 ledger note
about a seed-900115 diagnostic-plot crash quarantine), not something this measurement fixes; it
removes one already-excluded row from the sum, negligible at n=4800.

## 5. Residual factor vs RHS2 — does it close the gap?

| | LHS2 | RHS2 | ratio LHS2/RHS2 | residual factor X = RHS2/LHS2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **original** | 0.0050077 ± 0.0001162 | 0.0145130 ± 0.0004529 | 0.3450 ± 0.0093 | **2.898 ± 0.078** |
| **C1-corrected** | 0.0058013 ± 0.0001476 | 0.0145130 ± 0.0004529 | 0.3997 ± 0.0110 | **2.502 ± 0.069** |

(SEs propagated in quadrature on the ratios; RHS2 held fixed at its banked value — see caveat below.)

**Verdict: PARTIALLY closes the gap, and only by the previously-known amount.** The correction
recovers **13.6% of LHS2's own value**, moving the LHS2/RHS2 ratio from 34.5% to 40.0% of unity —
about 8% of the *total* deficit (65.5 percentage points → 60.0 percentage points). The residual
factor shrinks from **2.898× to 2.502×**, essentially reproducing the C2_star_review.md
elimination-derived figure "X_id = R_pred/obs = 2.506" (line 127) via an independent estimator —
**this is a confirmation of an already-known residual, not new closure.** The task's framing
("the unexplained factor was ~x2.5") already refers to the *post*-S̄_φ-correction residual from
prior session work; this measurement reproduces that number by a different, more principled
(self-normalized importance sampling over the *full* 200-draw denominator rather than a harmonic
mean over only the accepted subset) route, and stands as an independent confirmation that ~2.5×
is real and stable across methods — **it does not shrink further.**

## 6. ESS and tail-share diagnostics

Pooled over all 24 seeds, bt arm (2261 F0-accepted rows, 2221 LIVE, 1828 with strictly positive
`(1-w2)` weight; 4800 total drawn latents for the denominator):

| weight set | N (nonzero) | Kish ESS | ESS/N | top-0.9% share | top-5% share |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **original**, `(1-w2)` | 1828 | 668.2 | **0.366** | 4.3% | 22.7% |
| **corrected**, `(1-w2)/S̄_φ(z)` | 1828 | 586.0 | **0.321** | 6.2% | 27.4% |
| **denominator**, `1/S̄_φ(z)` (all 4800 drawn) | 4800 | 4740.0 | **0.988** | 1.5% | — |

(bc arm: essentially the same pattern — ESS/N 0.310 → 0.274, top-0.9% share 5.0% → 7.1%. Full
per-seed tables: `c1_bt_perseed.csv`, `c1_bc_perseed.csv` in the scratchpad above.)

**The reweighting does make the tail modestly worse (ESS/N drops ~12% relative), but it is not
alarming.** The denominator (`1/S̄_φ` over all 4800 drawn latents) is essentially flat — over this
fleet `z_true ∈ [0.005, 0.34]` only (well inside the low-z regime), `S̄_φ(z) ∈ [0.481, 0.989]`,
never near zero, so `1/S̄_φ ≤ 2.08` everywhere and its ESS/N = 0.988 (no heavy tail is introduced
by the correction itself). The pre-existing numerator tail (already present in the *original*
LHS2 estimator, ESS/N=0.366) is mildly amplified because the low-`(1-w2)` (high-w2, well-localized,
low-z) events that already dominate the sum also happen to sit at slightly lower `S̄_φ`... no —
inspection shows the modest increase (0.366→0.321) is small enough that **the corrected LHS2 number
is trustworthy at the precision quoted; it is not being carried by a one-or-two-event artifact.**

## 7. The "73%"/"56%" warning: verified, but it describes the OTHER side

The task quotes the searcher's warning — *"73% of accepted rows have `L_cat_with_bh == 0`... top
~0.9% of events carry ~56% of the mean"* — as a caveat on trusting this estimator. **Tracing it to
its source (`stageL_p32d_fresh_search_20260827.md:71-90`, §F-A) shows this statistic is measured
over the RHS2 (completion/class-Ḡ) side** ("Over 60 banked class-Ḡ chunks... scored with the
with-BH columns"), **not the LHS2/class-G b0i2d side that the C1 correction touches.** I confirmed
this is real — pooling all 449 available `ca_rhs_work/score_chunk*` RHS-side chunks (84,077 F0-accepted
rows, a much larger sample than the source's 60-chunk/11,239-row check):

| statistic | source doc (60 chunks, N=11,239) | this check (449 chunks, N=84,077) |
|---|---|---|
| `L_cat_with_bh == 0` frac | 73% | **72.7%** |
| `w2 > 0.5` frac | 0.79% | **0.81%** |
| top ~0.9% share of the `w2`-weighted mean | 56% | **54.8%** |
| Kish ESS/N (pooled) | not reported | **0.0228** (ESS ≈ 1919 of 84,077; at the registered N≈23,040 F0-accepted scale, ESS ≈ 526) |

**Both figures reproduce closely on an independent, larger pool — the searcher's RHS2-side warning
is correct, but it does not apply to the object §4-6 of this report measures.** LHS2 (either
variant) is comparatively well-behaved (ESS/N ≈ 0.32–0.37); **RHS2 is the estimator that is
severely tail-dominated** (ESS/N ≈ 0.023, roughly 16× worse).

**This is a load-bearing caveat for §5's residual-factor verdict.** RHS2's banked SEM (±0.00045293,
implicitly assuming an effective N close to its nominal 25,600/~23,000-accepted draws) is very
likely a substantial underestimate: a naive √N SEM computed at the *true* Kish-effective N (≈500–600,
scaling the 0.0228 ratio to the registered fleet size) would be inflated by roughly
√(23,000/550) ≈ **6.5×**, i.e. plausibly ±0.003 rather than ±0.00045. That does not by itself
explain the ~2.5× residual (0.003 ≪ 0.0087, the corrected-LHS2-to-RHS2 gap), but it means **the
"2.5σ_comb"-style significance language used earlier in this thread for the RHS2 side should be
treated with real skepticism** — this measurement did not attempt to recompute RHS2's true
tail-aware SE (that requires the actual registered 128-chunk fleet, not this ad hoc superset, and
is out of zero-compute scope), but flags that it has almost certainly not converged to Gaussian
behavior at N=25,600 given a mean-carrying tail this concentrated.

## 8. Bottom line

1. **C1 mechanism is real and reproducible**, confirmed twice now by independent methods
   (harmonic-ratio division in `venue_drift_adjudication.py`; self-normalized importance sampling
   here): correcting the class-G venue's double-application of `S̄_φ(z)` raises LHS2 by
   **15.7% ± 0.7%** (bt), consistent across arms.
2. **It explains only ~14% of the total observed LHS2/RHS2 deficit** (ratio 0.345→0.400 of unity);
   the residual factor shrinks from **2.898× to 2.502×**, matching the already-banked "X_id=2.506"
   figure from prior forensic work almost exactly. **The correction does not close the ~×2.5 gap —
   it was already accounted for.**
3. **The reweighted estimator is trustworthy at the precision quoted** — ESS/N ≈ 0.32–0.37 for
   both LHS2 variants, no catastrophic tail, denominator essentially flat (`z_true` stays in
   [0.005, 0.34] where `S̄_φ` never drops below 0.48).
4. **The severe "73%/56%" tail-domination warning is real but describes RHS2, not LHS2** —
   independently confirmed on a 7.5×-larger pool (72.7%/54.8%, matching closely). RHS2's quoted
   SEM is likely underestimated by an order-~6× given its Kish ESS/N ≈ 0.023; the true statistical
   weight of the "2.5–2.9×" gap against RHS2 is therefore weaker than the naive SEM comparison
   implies, though not weak enough on its own to plausibly close the gap.
5. **The residual ~×2.5 factor remains UNATTRIBUTED.** This measurement neither locates it nor
   materially shrinks it; the stageL search document's own next-step candidates (RHS2-side
   completion-class mass-law mismatch, C1′'s "tail-weighted vs marginal" distinction) remain the
   live threads.
