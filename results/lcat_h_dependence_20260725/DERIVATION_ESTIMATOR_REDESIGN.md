## 0. Executive summary and decision table

The established mechanism (SYNTHESIS): `L_cat` is a self-normalized ratio-of-sums over the
per-event candidate ball. A ball containing only foreground impostors still yields an O(1)
likelihood whose h-shape follows the impostors; the catalogue-vs-dark weighting
(`β_G·L_cat` vs `B_num` inside `p_i = (β_G L_cat + B_num)/D`) is global, so an event with
collectively implausible candidates cannot defer to the completion term. D1's falsifiable
scoping statement: only interventions that change *which hosts carry numerator weight* can
de-rail.

Two candidate redesigns, both derived below from the single exhaustive per-event marginal:

| | New formula (one line) | De-rails? (measured, 12 instrumented events) | #29 limit continuous? | Proper likelihood? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Variant 1** — absolute-mass marginal (Chen 2018 / gwcosmo v2 LOS structure) | `p_i(h) = [ A_i(h) + B_num,i(h) ] / D(h)`, `A_i = Σ_ball w_g N_g(h) / n̄_w(h)`, `n̄_w = Σ_glob w_g D_g / β_G` | **Yes — fully.** Rail slopes −28.4, −26.0, −20.6, −6.9 → all 12 events within **[−0.85, +1.18]** | **Yes** (`A_i → 0` continuously; empty ball gives exactly `B_num/D`) | **Yes** (it *is* the marginal) |

pipeline's self-normalized `L_cat` kept — as Variant 2 is specified — it is a genuinely
different, and measurably inferior, estimator (§4.3).

**Key structural fact discovered during this derivation:** Variant 1 is algebraically
**identical to the pipeline's existing (dormant) `"volume_global"` normalization mode**
(`p_Di` lines 2103–2120): `A_i/D ≡ w_G · L_cat_global` exactly. The 2026-07-01/02

`bayesian_statistics.py:2096`) **predates** both the G2a completion-sky-marginal fix and the
issue-#29 zero-host fallback (2026-07-10) — it evaluated the global catalogue term inside a
broken mixture and is therefore confounded (SYNTHESIS follow-up 2; D2 §6 last bullet). No
prior verdict on the current stack contradicts Variant 1. D1 §5's "global mode still rails"
measured `d ln L_cat/dh` **in isolation**; in the assembled mixture the global-mode

rail slope is irrelevant — this is exactly the "defer to the completion term" behavior the
current estimator lacks.

**Recommendation to the author:** approve **Variant 1**. Variant 2 is documented to protocol

## 1. Common derivation: the exhaustive per-event host marginal

### 1.1 Generative model (matches the injection pipeline)

An EMRI event is generated by:
1. drawing a host from the universe's galaxy population with probability ∝ its per-galaxy
   EMRI rate weight — for catalogued galaxies this is exactly the simulation draw weight
   `w_g = R_eff_per_mbh(M_g)/(1+z_g)` (`_rate_weight`, `bayesian_statistics.py:535–558`;
   identical to `draw_rate_weighted_hosts`);
2. emitting a signal at `z_host`, observed as data `x_i = (φ̂, θ̂, d̂_L, σ_dL [, M̂_z, σ_Mz])`;
3. surviving the deterministic detection cut (SNR ≥ 20), a function of the *data*.

The hypothesis space for "which galaxy hosted event i" is exhaustive and mutually
exclusive: {each catalogued galaxy g} ∪ {some uncatalogued (dark) galaxy}. No other
hypothesis exists; the marginal over it is the per-event likelihood.

### 1.2 The marginal

Let `h` be the Hubble hypothesis. Conditioning on detection (Mandel, Farr & Gair 2019,
arXiv:1809.02063, Eq. 6 ff.: `P(det|x_i)=1` for detected data, so no `p_det` enters any
numerator; one selection factor `α(h)` divides the whole marginal):

```
p(x_i | h, det) = [ Σ_{g ∈ cat} w_g ∫ p(x_i | z, Ω_g, M_g; h) p_g(z) dz
                    + ∫∫ ρ_dark(z, Ω; h) p(x_i | z, Ω; h) dz dΩ ] / α(h)          (1)
```

- Catalogue term: the discrete sum over catalogued galaxies, each with prior weight `w_g`
  and redshift-uncertainty kernel `p_g(z)` (the volume-deconvolved kernel as implemented,
  which is exactly h-invariant — D1 §2 fact 2; kernel choice is NOT changed here).
  `N ≈ 0` outside the candidate ball `B_i`, so the sum may be restricted to `B_i`
  (Gray 2020 A9 numerator self-truncates; D2 §3 row 1 — set restriction is harmless
  *for the numerator*).
- Dark term: the uncatalogued hosts form a continuum with rate-weighted comoving density
  `ρ_dark(z) = n̄_w · (1 − f(z)) · (dV_c/dz)/(1+z)` per unit z per sr, where `f(z)` is the
  catalogue completeness **of the same pruned, rate-weighted catalogue population** (the
  identical `completeness` call the injection generator uses — bit-for-bit consistency,
  `precompute_missing_completion_denominator` docstring) and
  `n̄_w` is the mean rate-weight density of galaxies per comoving volume
  (Option A constant-comoving-density assumption, documented at
  `precompute_completion_denominator:649–656`).
- Selection: `α(h) = Σ_{g ∈ cat} w_g P_det(g;h) + ∫∫ ρ_dark P_det = n̄_w·[β_G(h) + β_Ḡ(h)]
  = n̄_w · D(h)`, using the catalogue-realizes-population identity
  `Σ_cat w_g P_det(d_L(z_g;h)) ≈ n̄_w β_G(h)` (Gray 2020, discrete Monte-Carlo realisation
  of `β_G = ∫ f P_det dV_c/(1+z)`; this identity is the code's own Option A statement,
  `precompute_global_catalog_selection:903–913`).

**Double-counting guard.** A catalogued galaxy appears once in the discrete sum and is
*excluded* from the continuum by the factor `(1 − f(z))`: `f(z)` is by definition the
catalogued fraction of the rate-weighted population, so catalogue + dark tile the population
exactly (`f + (1−f) = 1` at every z, per pixel in the sky-aware path). This is the same
tiling `β_G = D − β_Ḡ` already enforces on the selection side. No galaxy is counted twice;
no galaxy is dropped.

Dividing numerator and denominator of (1) by the single constant `n̄_w` gives the
implementable form:

```
p_i(h) = [ A_i(h) + B_num,i(h) ] / D(h)                                            (2)
A_i(h)     = (1/n̄_w(h)) Σ_{g ∈ B_i} w_g N_g(h)         [absolute catalogue mass]
B_num,i(h) = ∫ (1−f_k(z)) p_GW,iso(z;h) (dV_c/dz)/(1+z) dz   [as coded, unchanged]
D(h)       = ∫ ⟨P_det⟩_sky (dV_c/dz)/(1+z) dz                [as coded, unchanged]
```

with the calibration constant estimated from the catalogue itself:

```
n̄_w(h) = Σ_glob(h) / β_G(h),      Σ_glob(h) = Σ_{g: z_g < z_max(h)} w_g P_det(d_L(z_g;h))   (3)
```

— which is **exactly the already-precomputed pair** `_global_cat_denom_no_bh[h]` /
`_beta_G_table[h]` (`precompute_global_catalog_selection`, `bayesian_statistics.py:895–1030`;
`β_G = D − β_Ḡ`, `:2085–2088`).

**There is no self-normalization anywhere in (2):** the catalogue sum enters with its
absolute mass. `β_G` no longer multiplies the catalogue term as a separate factor — its
only surviving role is inside the calibration constant `n̄_w` (equivalently: the Gray
identity "catalogue-denominator h-shape ≡ β_G h-shape" is restored by construction, D2 §2.1
fact 3, so the two cancel and one global `1/D(h)` survives — the MFG single-normalization
structure). The completeness fraction `f(z)` survives only where it belongs: inside
`B_num`/`β_Ḡ` as the dark-population density weight. There is no per-event scalar `f_i`
mixing weight anywhere (the CFH18-style `f·p_cat + (1−f)·p_miss` mixture of Fishbach 2019
Eq. 3 is an equivalent *normalized-prior* presentation of the same marginal; the
absolute-mass presentation (2) is chosen because it needs no per-event prior normalization
and exposes the #29 limit continuously).

### 1.3 What the current estimator does instead (the defect, restated in these terms)

The production `p_i = (β_G · L_cat + B_num)/D` with
`L_cat = Σ_ball w N / Σ_ball w D` replaces the universal conversion constant `n̄_w` by the
**event-local** quantity `Σ_ball w_g D_g(h) / β_G(h)`. Dimensionally consistent, but
scale-inconsistent: the per-galaxy → per-volume conversion becomes event-dependent, inflating
the catalogue term by

```
S_i(h) = Σ_glob(h) / Σ_ball,i(h)      (measured at h=0.73: 9.4×10³ … 9.0×10⁷, Table T3)
```

relative to the marginal (2). This factor is what lets an impostor-only ball carry O(1)
weight against `B_num`: it *forces* the in-catalogue posterior mass to sum to the global
prior `β_G/D` regardless of how implausible the ball's candidates are. That is precisely
D1 §7's "the estimator never gets to say my candidates are collectively implausible".

## 3. VARIANT 1 — absolute-mass marginal

### 3.1 Protocol item 2 — NEW formula

Replace the event assembly (only) by Eq. (2)+(3):

```
p_i(h) = [ A_i(h) + B_num,i(h) ] / D(h)

A_i(h) = Σ_{g∈B_i} w_g N_g(h) / n̄_w(h),     n̄_w(h) = Σ_glob(h) / β_G(h)
```

per channel (no-BH: `Σ_glob = _global_cat_denom_no_bh`; with-BH:
`_global_cat_denom_with_bh`). `N_g`, `p_g(z)` (volume_deconv kernel), `w_g`, `B_num`,
`D(h)`, `β_Ḡ(h)` are all **unchanged**. The per-host `D_g` and the local sum
`Σ_ball w_g D_g` no longer enter `p_i`.

**Algebraic identity with existing code:** `A_i/D = (β_G/D)·(Σ w N / Σ_glob)
= w_G · L_cat_global` — i.e. the NEW formula is exactly the existing
`"global"`/`"volume_global"` branch of `p_Di` (`:2103–2120`) feeding the existing mixture
line (`:2263–2266`). Variant 1 is therefore *adoption of `volume_global` as the production
normalization mode*, justified this time from the marginal (1) rather than as an ad-hoc
mode, and evaluated on a stack where the #29 fallback and G2a sky-marginal fixes exist
(the 2026-07-01/02 rejection predates both — confounded, see §0).

Optionally (secondary, D2 Rank-1 hygiene): none of `S_i`, `Σ_ball wD` survives, so D2's
ball-restricted-denominator defect is closed by construction, not by a separate fix.

### 3.3 Protocol item 4 — Dimensional analysis (exhaustive; the commensurability point)

Units bookkeeping (`[X]⁻¹` ≡ per unit GW data-space volume, i.e. per (φ, θ, d_L-fraction);
`sr` factors written explicitly; `yr⁻¹` is the EMRI-rate unit carried by `R_eff`, with the
arbitrary overall `C_NORM` cancelling in every ratio):

| Quantity | Expression | Units |
|---|---|---|
| `N_g` | ∫ (GW pdf in (φ,θ,d_L/d̂)) × (normalized z-pdf) dz | `[X]⁻¹` |
| `w_g` | `R_eff(M_g)/(1+z_g)` | `yr⁻¹` |
| `Σ_ball w N` | | `yr⁻¹·[X]⁻¹` |
| `P_det`, `D_g` | probabilities | 1 |
| `Σ_glob(h)` | `Σ w_g P_det` | `yr⁻¹` |
| `D(h)`, `β_G`, `β_Ḡ` | `∫ p_det (dV_c/dz)/(1+z) dz` | `Mpc³ sr⁻¹` |
| `n̄_w = Σ_glob/β_G` | rate-weight density | `yr⁻¹ · sr · Mpc⁻³` |
| **`A_i = Σ w N / n̄_w`** | | **`[X]⁻¹ · Mpc³ sr⁻¹`** |
| `B_num` | `∫ (1−f) [N(d̂frac)·sinθ/4π] (dV_c/dz)/(1+z) dz` | `[X]⁻¹ · Mpc³ sr⁻¹` ✓ |
| `p_i = (A+B)/D` | | `[X]⁻¹` ✓ (a proper GW-data density, event-comparable) |

**`A_i` and `B_num` have identical units — the catalogue and dark terms are commensurable
by construction.** This was THE historical failure point; note the old form was also
*dimensionally* consistent (`β_G·L_cat` has `B_num`'s units too) — the failure was in the
*scale*, where the conversion constant was event-local (§1.3). Dimensional analysis alone
could never have caught it; the scale check below does.

**h-scale check (quantitative, new this session).** In flat ΛCDM, `dV_c/dz = h⁻³ f(z;Ω_m)`
exactly, so `D`, `β_G`, `β_Ḡ`, `B_num` each carry `h⁻³`, which cancels in `B_num/D` and
`β_Ḡ/D`. The discrete sums `Σ w N`, `Σ_glob` carry **no** `h⁻³` (fixed galaxy count). For
`A_i/D` to be free of the spurious scale, `n̄_w(h)` must supply `h⁺³` — physically: a fixed
number of galaxies occupies comoving volume ∝ h⁻³, so the number *density* ∝ h³. Measured
on the EXP-40 venue (`variant_slope_tables.py` T3):
`d ln Σ_glob/dh = +0.364`, `d ln β_G/dh = −3.37` ⇒ `d ln n̄_w/dh = +3.73`, vs the pure
`h³` slope `3/h = 4.11` at h=0.73. The `h³` is present as required; the residual −0.38/h
is the P_det-shape realization mismatch between the point-evaluated catalogue sum and the
kernel-free `β_G` integral (clustering + σ_z-smearing asymmetry) — a per-event ln-slope
contamination of ≤0.4/h, i.e. **two orders of magnitude below the −20…−55/h rail** it
replaces, and shared (event-independent), so it cannot re-grade by depth. (Tracked as risk
R4, §7.)

### 3.4 Protocol item 5 — Limiting cases

**(a) Complete catalogue, f→1 everywhere.** `1−f ≡ 0 ⇒ B_num = 0, β_Ḡ = 0, β_G = D,
n̄_w = Σ_glob/D`. Then
`p_i = A_i/D = Σ_ball w N / Σ_glob(h)` — the **full-catalogue** ratio-of-sums of Gray 2020
A9 / Gair 2023 Eq. 15 (numerator self-truncated to the ball, selection denominator over all
N_gal). Pure catalogue statistics recovered, in the reference form — *not* the current
ball-local form, exactly as all three references require (D2 §3 row 2).

**(b) Empty / all-impostor ball.** Empty ball: `A_i = 0` (empty sum) ⇒ `p_i = B_num/D`
identically — the #29 fallback **emerges as the continuous limit**, no branch. Impostor
ball: `A_i` is small but nonzero; measured at h=0.73 (Table T1 evidence, catalogue:dark
weight `A/B`): ev 880 `3.9×10⁻⁶`, ev 2556 `1.7×10⁻¹⁰`, ev 3070 `6.3×10⁻¹⁰`, ev 1580
`1.4×10⁻¹⁰⁸`, vs the current estimator's `25.3`, `3.1×10⁻³`, `7.2×10⁻⁴`, `1.5×10⁻¹⁰⁰`
after β_G-weighting — i.e. the impostor suppression the current form lacks by factors
of `S_i = Σ_glob/Σ_ball ≈ 10⁴–10⁸`. Removing one galaxy from a 1-galaxy impostor ball
changes `p_i` by a relative `A/B ~ 10⁻⁶` — continuity in the strong (quantitative) sense.

**(c) Shallow venue: p_det ≈ 1, well-matched hosts (the seed600 regression prediction).**
With `P_det ≡ 1` over the catalogue's support: `D_g → 1 ⇒ Σ_ball w D → Σ_ball w`
(h-constant), and `Σ_glob(h) → Σ_{z<z_max(h)} w → W_tot` (h-constant once the horizon
clears the catalogue depth — true on seed600, catalogue z ≲ 0.3, horizon z ≳ 1). Then
current: `p_i^cur = β_G(h)·[Σ w N(h)]/(Σ_ball w · D(h)) + B/D`;
Variant 1: `p_i^V1 = β_G(h)·[Σ w N(h)]/(Σ_glob · D(h)) + B/D`.
The two catalogue terms differ by the **h-constant** per-event factor
`Σ_ball w / Σ_glob w` — a pure per-event rescaling, invisible to the per-event *shape* and
hence to the joint posterior, **whenever the catalogue term dominates the mixture in both
forms**. On seed600 (median z≈0.05, hosts well-matched, ball contains the true host,
`N_g` large) the well-matched-event pattern of Table T1 applies (ev 373: `A/B = 45` even
after the 10⁵ suppression) — catalogue-dominated in both forms ⇒ per-event h-shapes agree
⇒ **predicted seed600 A/B outcome: MAP unchanged within tolerance** (gate: §6.2). Honest
caveat: events sitting at intermediate `A/B` under V1 (like ev 899, `A/B = 0.12`) acquire a
completion admixture the current form suppresses; on a shallow venue with high `f(z)` at
low z, `B_num` is small precisely where those events live, so the admixture is
second-order — but this is exactly what the seed600 must-not-change gate exists to verify,
not assume.

**(d) H0-independence sanity (uninformative event).** Let the GW window span the full
analysis volume with `p(x_i|z,Ω)` → constant `c` (σ_dL/d_L → ∞). Then
`Σ w N → c·Σ w` (h-constant; the kernel `p_g` is h-invariant, D1 fact 2), so
`A_i → c·Σw/n̄_w(h) ∝ c·h⁻³` (from n̄_w ∝ h³); `B_num → c·h⁻³·(h-indep. shape)`;
`D ∝ h⁻³·⟨P_det⟩-shape`. The `h⁻³` cancels exactly and
`p_i(h) ∝ c / [∫⟨P_det(d_L(z;h))⟩ g(z)/(1+z) dz]` — **no candidate-set-dependent tilt
survives**; the only residual h-shape is the selection factor `1/⟨P_det⟩(h)` **shared by
every event**, which is the MFG-correct behavior of a detection-conditioned likelihood (it
is exactly flat in the p_det≈1 venue, and in general is the price of analyzing shape-only
at fixed N_det). Contrast the current estimator, which in this limit still assigns the
event the *event-specific* monotone shape `β_G(h)/Σ_ball w D(h)` (D2 §4.2) — the failed
version of this sanity check. (In practice `use_detection` cuts σ_dL/d_L ≥ 0.10 events
before this limit is approached, `:2300–2314`.)

## 4. VARIANT 2 — Gray-style per-event membership mixture

### 4.1 Protocol item 2 — NEW formula

Keep `L_cat` (self-normalized local ratio, as computed) and `B_num` (as computed); replace
the global `β_G/D` weighting by per-event posterior membership odds. From the marginal (2),
the posterior probability that event i's host is catalogued is

```
P(G | x_i, h) = A_i(h) / [A_i(h) + B_num,i(h)]  ≡ λ_i(h),
P(Ḡ | x_i, h) = 1 − λ_i(h),        odds  λ/(1−λ) = A_i/B_num,i            (4)
```

(computable from existing pieces: `A_i/B = w_G·L_cat_global·D/B_num`), and

```
p_i^V2(h) = λ_i(h) · L_cat(h) + (1 − λ_i(h)) · L_dark(h)                   (5)
```

with two candidate dark-branch pairings: `L_dark = B_num/D` (continuous with the #29
fallback) or `L_dark = L_comp = B_num/β_Ḡ` (the diagnostic completion likelihood,
`:2272–2274`).

**Nomenclature caution:** Gray 2020's own mixture weight `p(G|D_GW,H0)` (Eq. A10–A12 block)
is conditioned on *detection only*, not on the event data `x_i` — that is exactly the
current pipeline's `w_G = β_G/D`. A weight conditioned on `x_i` is *not* in Gray; Variant 2
is the "Gray-style" mixture only in shape.

### 4.2 Protocol items 3–4 — References / dimensional analysis

References: identical corpus to §3.2 — because the odds (4) are *derived from Variant 1's
absolute masses*; no reference constructs (5) with self-normalized components (this is a
finding, and Variant 2's central weakness). Dimensional analysis: `λ_i` is dimensionless
(ratio of commensurable masses, §3.3); `L_cat` is `[X]⁻¹` and `B_num/D` is `[X]⁻¹` ✓;
`L_comp = B_num/β_Ḡ` is `[X]⁻¹` ✓. Both pairings are dimensionally consistent; the
`L_comp` pairing divides by `β_Ḡ → 0` as `f → 1` (guarded in code but a removable
singularity the `B/D` pairing does not have).

### 4.3 The Variant-1 ↔ Variant-2 relationship (exact statement)

Substituting `L_cat = n̄_w A_i / Σ_ball wD` and `L_dark = B/β_Ḡ` into (5):

```
p_i^V2 = [ n̄_w A_i² / Σ_ball wD  +  B² / β_Ḡ ] / (A_i + B)
   vs.
p_i^V1 = (A_i + B) / D
```

**V2 ≡ V1 iff the mixture components themselves are the absolute ratios**, i.e. iff one
replaces `L_cat → A_i/(λ_i D) = (A_i+B)/D` and `L_dark → B/((1−λ_i)D) = (A_i+B)/D` — at
which point (5) collapses to the tautology `p_i = (A_i+B)/D` and Variant 2 *is* Variant 1
rewritten. With the pipeline's self-normalized pieces kept (as specified), equality would
require `n̄_w A²/Σ_ball wD + B²/β_Ḡ = (A+B)²/D` for all h — generically false. The genuine
differences, precisely located:

1. the surviving **ball-local self-normalization** `Σ_ball w D(h)` re-enters through the
   `λ_i·L_cat` term with weight λ_i — so wherever λ_i is not ≪1, the impostor h-shape
   (and D2's Rank-1 ball-denominator defect) survives with O(λ_i) weight;
2. the **squared structure** (`A²`, `B²` instead of `A`, `B`): V2 is a self-weighted mean
   of two likelihood *ratios*, not a marginal — it double-uses the data (once inside λ_i,
   once inside L_cat), and is therefore not a likelihood derivable from the exhaustive
   marginal (no reference constructs it; a P-P harness would be its only defensible
   justification);
3. the **dark-branch pairing ambiguity** (B/D vs B/β_Ḡ) — V1 has no such free choice.

### 4.4 Protocol item 5 — Limiting cases (with measurements)

- **(a) f→1:** `B → 0, λ → 1 ⇒ p_i^V2 → L_cat` — recovers the *current local* estimator,
  NOT the references' full-catalogue form. Fails the reference-limit check that V1 passes.
- **(b) Empty ball:** `A = 0, λ = 0 ⇒ p_i^V2 → L_dark`. Continuous with #29 **only** for
  the `B/D` pairing; the `L_comp` pairing changes the fallback events' likelihood by the
  h-dependent factor `D/β_Ḡ` — a silent physics change to 58% of deep-venue events.
- **(c) Shallow venue:** λ → 1 (catalogue-dominated) ⇒ `p_i^V2 → L_cat` = current
  estimator's catalogue term up to the (then nearly h-flat) `β_G/D` factor ⇒ seed600
  predicted unchanged, same as V1.
- **(d) Uninformative event:** `L_cat → Σw·c/Σ_ball wD`, an *event-specific* h-shape
  (the D2 §4.2 residual factor) entering with weight λ_i — the sanity check is only
  passed to the extent λ_i is small. Weaker than V1.
- **Measured deep-venue slopes** (T2): impostor balls flatten (λ ~ 10⁻⁶: ev 880 −0.4,
  3344 −0.8, 775 −0.9), **but mixed-membership events retain a residual rail**:
  ev 899 (λ=0.105) **−13.2**, ev 1404 (λ=6×10⁻⁴ but λ(h) itself steep, `d ln λ/dh = −24`)
  **−8.0/−8.5**, ev 3112 (λ=0.47) −2.7 — for both pairings. The residual comes from
  items 1–2 above: `λ_i(h)·L_cat(h)` compounds the odds' h-shape with the self-normalized
  ratio's h-shape. Variant 2 de-rails the extreme events but leaves a depth-graded
  intermediate population railed at up to ~½ the current worst slopes.

### 4.5 Verdict on Variant 2

Not recommended. It is strictly dominated by Variant 1 on every criterion measured here
(derivability, reference support, limit (a), limit (d), residual mixed-regime rail), and
offers no compensating advantage — its only appeal (keeping `L_cat` "as computed") is
cosmetic, since V1 also reuses every computed ingredient (`Σ w N`, `Σ_glob`, `β_G`, `B_num`,
`D`). If the author nevertheless wants a membership diagnostic, `λ_i(h)` from (4) is
worth EMITTING as a per-event diagnostic column under Variant 1 (it is the posterior
host-is-catalogued probability and directly instruments D1's mechanism), without using it
as a mixture weight.

## 7. Risks and open questions for the author (ranked)

1. **[HIGH] Sensitivity of `A_i` to the rate-weight model `w_g` (and the mass prune).**
   Under self-normalization, only *relative* weights within a ball mattered; under the
   absolute form, the catalogue:dark balance `A/B` inherits the absolute scale of
   `R_eff(M_g)` through both `Σ w N` and `n̄_w`. The `C_NORM`-type overall constant still
