\begin{SummaryBox}
\textbf{Summary}

The paper diagnoses and repairs a railed Hubble-constant posterior in an independently implemented Gray-family galaxy-catalogue dark-siren estimator (simulated LISA EMRI campaign, injected $h = 0.73$, real GLADE+ catalogue). Its diagnostic arc is strong: three normalization defects under one prior-consistency invariant; a falsifiable sign-flip prediction (rail at $0.86 \to$ flip to $0.60 \to$ interior peak at $0.73$) observed on identical data; a two-factor ablation separating kernel consistency from the denominator choice; and an Eddington-in-$z$ $\sigma_z^2$ bias law, verified across a factor 11 in $\sigma_z^2$, that collapses frequentist coverage for the bare-Gaussian kernel while the volume-deconvolved kernel calibrates close to nominal in the clean single-host limit. The mathematics is unusually well verified: two independent panel passes recomputed essentially every quoted coefficient and found no mathematical error.

Three problem classes cap the recommendation. (1) The manuscript is literally incomplete: the full-scale confirmation of the headline de-rail is an explicit \texttt{[RESULT PENDING]} placeholder (realdata.tex:73); the demonstration rests on a 494-event subsample and a seven-point grid whose injected truth coincides with the grid midpoint. (2) Claim scope exceeds evidence in the significance backbone: the abstract designates $\sigma_z/z \sim 0.7$ as ``the regime probed here'' while the coverage evidence lives at $\sigma_z/z \sim 0.1$--$0.2$; the introduction misattributes $\sigma_z^2$ suppression to all three defects; one Section~6 sentence (``3331 of 3355 \ldots numerator evaluates to zero'') is a demonstrable misreading of a diagnostic log; and the same-round proof red-team fails closed (\texttt{gaps\_found}). (3) The literature positioning must be rebuilt: Cross-Parkin et al.\ 2025 --- published, uncited, known-truth, photometric, volume-prior --- falsifies the introduction's implicit universal, and the citation horizon ends mid-2023.

None of the required repairs replaces the central result: the rail-is-a-curable-artifact conclusion, the Eddington-in-$z$ law, the coverage-collapse demonstration, and the prior-consistency invariant all survive, and the negative novelty claim survives adversarial search under its full three-part conjunction. The narrowed, completed paper is a genuine MNRAS methods contribution. Major revision, not rejection.
\end{SummaryBox}

\begin{StrengthBox}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item A falsifiable diagnostic chain stated before the test and observed on identical data, with a clean two-factor ablation (kernel consistency de-rails; the local denominator removes the residual $+0.03$, consistent with the independently measured $-17$ per cent global tilt).
  \item Mechanism-level verification: $q(0.05)=38.1$, $K(0.25)=20.1$, $K_\mathrm{meas}=17$--$20$ stable to $\pm 8$ per cent across a factor 11 in $\sigma_z^2$; the non-perturbative regime identified and handled by exact quadrature.
  \item An independent, deterministic, released P--P/coverage harness (no production-code imports, paired seeds, regression-pinned) --- a community tool, not just supporting evidence.
  \item Exact term-by-term mapping onto Gray et al.\ (2020), finite at $f \to 1$, with every deviation declared deliberate.
  \item Unusual claim hygiene: per-number artifact sourcing, disclosed unfavourable items (d$t^2$ SNR-scale defect, timeout selection, $\Omega_m$ fiducial), explicit simulated-events scope statements.
  \item The pinned-version four-pipeline audit, honest in both directions (rail structurally excluded from \textsc{gwcosmo}; bare kernel nonetheless live there by unenforced declaration).
\end{enumerate}
\end{StrengthBox}

\section*{Major Issues}

\begin{MajorIssueBox}{REF-R001: Headline confirmation is an explicit [RESULT PENDING] placeholder (blocking)}
\textbf{Dimension:} completeness

\textbf{Location:} \texttt{paper\_a/sections/realdata.tex:73}; \texttt{conclusions.tex:73--77}

\textbf{Description:} The full-scale confirmation (all 3375 seed600 events, complete 38-value $h$ grid, volume-deconvolved default) is unfilled; all interior-peak evidence rests on a 494-event subsample over a seven-point grid ($\pm 0.03$ resolution near the truth). Merges REF-M002, REF-S002.

\textbf{Impact:} The central real-data demonstration cannot be finally adjudicated; if the full-grid posterior is not peaked near 0.73, the de-rail narrative must be revised.

\textbf{Suggested fix:} Fill the placeholder with the completed full-grid combined-posterior result (cluster jobs 5698617/5698618) before any submission decision; re-check the abstract against it.
\end{MajorIssueBox}

\begin{MajorIssueBox}{REF-R002: Abstract designates a regime ($\sigma_z/z \sim 0.7$) no evidence bundle occupies (blocking)}
\textbf{Dimension:} significance

\textbf{Location:} \texttt{abstract.tex:7--9, 30--35}; \texttt{coverage.tex:12--13}; \texttt{realdata.tex:71}; \texttt{conclusions.tex:44--50}

\textbf{Description:} The coverage calibration runs at $\sigma_z = 0.035$, median detected $z \sim 0.3$ ($\sigma_z/z \sim 0.1$--$0.2$, perturbative regime $\sigma_z q \sim 0.2$); the real-data sample is mostly near-spectroscopic. At the designated order-unity ratio the effect is non-perturbative ($\sigma_z q = 1.33$ at $z_g = 0.05$, the paper's own Appendix~B) and is never coverage-tested by anyone, including this paper. Merges REF-P001, REF-S001.

\textbf{Impact:} The central selling point (probing the order-unity validation gap) is not what the paper delivers; the gap it names remains open for its own harness too.

\textbf{Suggested fix:} Add a non-perturbative coverage run (hosts $z \sim 0.05$--$0.1$, $\sigma_z \sim 0.035$, catalogue-dominated), or scope abstract/introduction/conclusions to state which $\sigma_z/z$ each evidence bundle occupies and that the order-unity regime remains untested for every pipeline including this one.
\end{MajorIssueBox}

\begin{MajorIssueBox}{REF-M001: The ``3331 of 3355 events'' sentence is false as written (blocking)}
\textbf{Dimension:} correctness

\textbf{Location:} \texttt{realdata.tex:54}

\textbf{Description:} The quoted log value ``numerator=0.000'' is the \emph{fraction of quadrature weight outside the $p_\mathrm{det}$ grid} (zero means full coverage), not a vanishing in-catalogue numerator; the sentence contradicts \texttt{realdata.tex:9} (all events inside the grid), $w_G = 0.8175$, and the catalogue-only row of Table~2. Merges REF-R004, REF-P003.

\textbf{Impact:} The inference that the completion machinery carries most of the post-fix constraint is unsupported; the paper's own numbers show the opposite (catalogue-only mean 0.737 vs full 0.740). Correcting it \emph{strengthens} ``the in-catalogue information was present all along''.

\textbf{Suggested fix:} Rewrite to what the diagnostic measured; recast channel dominance as pre-fix ($10^3$--$10^5$ $B_\mathrm{num}$ inflation) vs post-fix (catalogue channel dominates); support any retained completion-dominance claim with a per-event channel decomposition.
\end{MajorIssueBox}

\begin{MajorIssueBox}{REF-L001: Cross-Parkin et al.\ 2025 uncited; validation-gap narrative falsified as stated (blocking)}
\textbf{Dimension:} literature\_context

\textbf{Location:} \texttt{introduction.tex:57--64}; \texttt{abstract.tex}; \texttt{codes.tex}; \texttt{conclusions.tex}

\textbf{Description:} Cross-Parkin et al.\ 2025 (PASA, arXiv:2502.17747) is a published known-truth photometric-redshift dark-siren bias study (Gray-family, comoving-volume prior, ensemble bias tests, outlier models, unbiased $H_0$ at $\sigma_z/z \lesssim 0.08$). It falsifies the introduction's implicit universal and strains the abstract's ``almost entirely spectroscopic''. The gap claim survives under its full three-part conjunction; their result corroborates the paper's repair.

\textbf{Impact:} The paper claims more novelty than it owns until the framing --- the significance backbone --- is rebuilt.

\textbf{Suggested fix:} Cite the work; rewrite the validation-gap narrative as a three-way taxonomy; restate the surviving gap precisely; re-derive the abstract's significance sentences from the narrowed claim.
\end{MajorIssueBox}

\begin{MajorIssueBox}{REF-L002: Citation horizon ends mid-2023; the negative claim lacks its adjacent-work map (blocking)}
\textbf{Dimension:} literature\_context

\textbf{Location:} \texttt{codes.tex:4--9, 39--43}

\textbf{Description:} At least five 2025--2026 works in the immediate niche are unexamined (gwcosmo Blinded MDC I; Borghi et al.\ 2026; the audited-but-uncited CHIMERA v2 code paper; Alfradique et al.\ 2025; VanWyngarden et al.\ 2025). All were verified as not filling the gap.

\textbf{Impact:} A negative universal at MNRAS needs ``here is the complete map and the cell is empty''; referees will name these works.

\textbf{Suggested fix:} Add a compact adjacent-known-truth-studies passage to Section~8 stating, for each, why it does not occupy the catalogue-dominated $\sigma_z/z \sim 0.7$ regime.
\end{MajorIssueBox}

\begin{MajorIssueBox}{REF-P004: Mechanism sentence physically wrong for two of the three defects (blocking)}
\textbf{Dimension:} correctness

\textbf{Location:} \texttt{introduction.tex:81--87} (also 100--104; \texttt{abstract.tex:7--9})

\textbf{Description:} ``Effects are second order in the redshift kernel width'' holds only for the kernel defect: the peak-density over-weight $2/(\sigma_\phi \sigma_\theta)$ contains no $\sigma_z$, and the $-17$ per cent global tilt is measured at the $\sigma_z = 0$ configuration. Their historical invisibility is a validation-configuration property (complete-catalogue mocks, code-vs-code common modes), which Section~8 documents correctly.

\textbf{Impact:} The misattribution props up the hazard claim's generality; ``calibrating perfectly on spectroscopic-quality validation inputs'' is demonstrated only for the kernel defect.

\textbf{Suggested fix:} Rewrite: $\sigma_z^2$ suppression for Defect~1 alone; configuration-based invisibility for Defects~2 and~3; qualify the ``calibrating perfectly'' clause.
\end{MajorIssueBox}

\begin{MajorIssueBox}{REF-X001: Proof red-team fails closed --- remaining theorem-scoping repairs (blocking)}
\textbf{Dimension:} technical\_soundness

\textbf{Location:} \texttt{review/PROOF-REDTEAM.md}; \texttt{estimators.tex} (secs.~4.1, 4.5); \texttt{appendix\_sky\_marginal.tex:95--99}; \texttt{framework.tex} vs \texttt{appendix\_beta\_g.tex}; \texttt{introduction.tex:100--104}

\textbf{Description:} The same-round adversarial proof audit reports \texttt{gaps\_found}: (a) the ``prior-consistent'' label for \texttt{local\_ratio} satisfies only requirement~(i) of the paper's own definition, and the ``every per-galaxy constant cancels row by row'' lemma is false as phrased; (b) the sky-marginal bound $\lesssim 10^{-5}$ drops the sub-percent $h$-smooth drift qualifier; (c) completeness notation $f(z,\Omega)$ vs $f(z,\Omega,h)$ is inconsistent; (d) the literal universal ``every independently implemented dark-siren pipeline'' is contradicted by the paper's own audit. (CLM-015 clamp and CLM-019 corollary are ledgered as REF-M004, REF-M005.) No mathematical counterexample was found.

\textbf{Impact:} A non-passing proof red-team forecloses any favorable recommendation; theorem clauses that outrun their proofs are never compatible with minor revision.

\textbf{Suggested fix:} Execute PROOF-REDTEAM.md follow-ups 1--6 verbatim; re-run the red-team to \texttt{status: passed}.
\end{MajorIssueBox}

\begin{MajorIssueBox}{REF-R003: ``Calibrated close to nominal'' scoped only by the clean single-host limit (major, non-blocking)}
\textbf{Dimension:} completeness

\textbf{Location:} \texttt{coverage.tex:75--77}; \texttt{abstract.tex:26--29}; \texttt{conclusions.tex:48--50}

\textbf{Description:} Full-machinery synthetic coverage is $0.40/0.54/0.82$ with residual $-0.013$ bias and MAP-vs-truth slope $\approx 0.35$; the attribution to the synthetic's completeness/interloper modelling is asserted without an ablation, and the real-data injected truth coincides with the grid midpoint, so truth recovery and mid-grid shrinkage are degenerate there. Merges REF-M007, REF-P002, REF-S006.

\textbf{Impact:} The adopted estimator would not pass, at nominal, the very coverage test the paper prescribes as a field prerequisite; a referee can turn the paper's standard against it.

\textbf{Suggested fix:} Scope the claim to the clean single-host limit at abstract/conclusion level; add a completeness/interloper ablation or one off-centre-truth full-machinery injection; note the midpoint coincidence.
\end{MajorIssueBox}

\begin{MajorIssueBox}{REF-S004: Significance framing weights the self-inflicted arc over the field-live result (major, non-blocking)}
\textbf{Dimension:} significance

\textbf{Location:} \texttt{abstract.tex:14--25}; \texttt{introduction.tex:100--104}; \texttt{conclusions.tex:36--47}

\textbf{Description:} Two of the three defects are implementation errors structurally excluded from all four audited pipelines --- the rail arc is prophylactic. The field-live result (bare-kernel Eddington bias in \textsc{gwcosmo} by unenforced declaration, coverage collapse) is the actual venue case; \texttt{codes.tex} states the split correctly, the abstract leads with the rail.

\textbf{Impact:} As framed, a referee can ask whether the core is a code post-mortem; rebalanced, the paper clears the MNRAS bar comfortably.

\textbf{Suggested fix:} Lead with the bias law, coverage collapse, and validation gap; present the rail arc as the exposing case study; scope the hazard sentence to the ex-ante reading.
\end{MajorIssueBox}
