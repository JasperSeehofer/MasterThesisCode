This appendix derives the volume-deconvolved host-redshift prior used by the
prior-consistent estimators of Section~\ref{sec:estimators} and the
$\sigma_z^2$ bias law obeyed by the bare-Gaussian kernel that it replaces,
and compares the law with the biases measured on the synthetic-universe suite.

\paragraph*{The deconvolved prior.}
The catalogue reports for galaxy $g$ an observed redshift $z_g$ with Gaussian
uncertainty $\sigma_z$, i.e.\ a \emph{likelihood}
$p(z_g \mid z) = \mathcal{N}(z_g;\,z,\sigma_z)$ for the true redshift $z$; a
probability density for $z$ additionally requires a population prior. Hosts
are drawn from the astrophysical event rate, whose detector-frame density per
unit true redshift is
$\mathrm{d}N/(\mathrm{d}t\,\mathrm{d}z) =
\mathcal{R}(z)\,(\mathrm{d}V_c/\mathrm{d}z)/(1+z)$, with $\mathcal{R}$ the
comoving source-frame rate density, $\mathrm{d}V_c/\mathrm{d}z$ the comoving
volume element \citep{Hogg:1999ad}, and $1/(1+z)$ the cosmological time
dilation of a source-frame rate \citep{Mandel:2018mve,Babak:2017tow}. For the
redshift-independent $\mathcal{R}$ of the rate model adopted here
(Section~\ref{sec:framework}), the prior shape is
\begin{equation}
  w_{\rm pop}(z) \;\equiv\; \frac{1}{1+z}\,\frac{\mathrm{d}V_c}{\mathrm{d}z} ,
  \label{eq:app:wpop}
\end{equation}
and Bayes' theorem gives the per-galaxy host-redshift posterior
\begin{equation}
  p_g(z) \;=\; \frac{\mathcal{N}(z_g;\,z,\sigma_z)\;w_{\rm pop}(z)}{Z_g},
  \quad
  Z_g = \!\!\int\limits_{\max(z_g - 4\sigma_z,\,0)}^{z_g + 4\sigma_z}\!\!
  \mathcal{N}(z_g;\,z',\sigma_z)\,w_{\rm pop}(z')\,\mathrm{d}z' .
  \label{eq:app:pgz}
\end{equation}
% src: docs/derivations/G2b_host_z_volume_prior.md Eq. (1.4); z >= 0 clamp per
% bayesian_statistics.py:1892-1898 (window matches B_num and D(h) lower limits)
Both $Z_g$ and the per-galaxy selection integral are evaluated by 50-point
Gauss--Legendre quadrature over the same window,
% src: docs/derivations/G2b_host_z_volume_prior.md §3.1; bayesian_statistics.py FIXED_QUAD_N=50
which is clamped at $z \ge 0$: for low-redshift hosts with $z_g < 4\sigma_z$
an unclamped window would extend to unphysical $z<0$, where the formal volume
element remains positive (the comoving distance enters squared) and $Z_g$
would silently accrue spurious prior mass --- bounded by
$\Phi(-z_g/\sigma_z)$ times an order-unity volume ratio, i.e.\ below a per
cent of $Z_g$ for $z_g/\sigma_z \gtrsim 2$, but removed exactly by the clamp.
% src: docs/derivations/G2b_host_z_volume_prior.md §3.3 item 1

Three properties make Eq.~\eqref{eq:app:pgz} the unique prior consistent with
the rest of the likelihood. (i)~\emph{Measure symmetry:} the same weight
$w_{\rm pop}(z)\,\mathrm{d}z$ of Eq.~\eqref{eq:app:wpop} appears exactly once in every redshift integral
of the estimator --- the in-catalogue numerator and its selection
denominator, the completion term, and the selection normalizations $D(h)$
and $\beta_{\bar G}(h)$ --- never zero times (the bare-Gaussian defect) and
never twice. $Z_g$ cancels in a single galaxy's numerator-to-denominator
ratio but deliberately not in the ratio of sums, where it assigns each galaxy
one unit of prior mass regardless of how much comoving volume lies under its
photometric error bar.
% src: docs/derivations/G2b_host_z_volume_prior.md §3.2
(ii)~\emph{Exact $h$-independence:} writing $E(z) = H(z)/H_0$,
$I(z) = \int_0^z \mathrm{d}z'/E(z')$, one has
$w_{\rm pop}(z; h) = h^{-3} g(z)$ with
$g(z) \propto I^2(z)/[E(z)(1+z)]$, so the $h^{-3}$ prefactor cancels between
the numerator of Eq.~\eqref{eq:app:pgz} and $Z_g$: the deconvolution injects
no spurious $h$-dependence, only the redshift \emph{shape} of the prior acts.
% src: docs/derivations/G2b_host_z_volume_prior.md Eq. (1.5)
(iii)~\emph{Spectroscopic limit:} as $\sigma_z \to 0$,
$\mathcal{N} \to \delta(z - z_g)$ and $p_g(z) \to \delta(z - z_g)$; the prior
becomes irrelevant and the kernel reduces continuously to the bare-Gaussian
(spectroscopic) case, consistent with the measured bias vanishing there.

\paragraph*{Eddington bias of the bare kernel.}
The bare-Gaussian kernel uses $\mathcal{N}(z;\,z_g,\sigma_z)$ in place of
Eq.~\eqref{eq:app:pgz}. Expanding $\ln w_{\rm pop}$ about $z_g$ and
completing the square, the true posterior is, to leading order, a Gaussian of
unchanged width recentred at
\begin{equation}
  \langle z \rangle = z_g + \sigma_z^2\, s(z_g) + \mathcal{O}(\sigma_z^4),
  \quad
  s(z) \equiv \frac{\mathrm{d}\ln w_{\rm pop}}{\mathrm{d}z}
  = \frac{2}{I E} - \frac{E'}{E} - \frac{1}{1+z},
  \label{eq:app:eddz}
\end{equation}
% src: docs/derivations/G2b_host_z_volume_prior.md Eqs. (2.1)-(2.2)
with $E'(z) = 3\Omega_{\rm m}(1+z)^2/(2E)$ for flat $\Lambda$CDM; at low
redshift $s \approx 2/z$. This is the classical Eddington--Malmquist
correction transcribed to redshift
\citep{MISSING:Eddington1913}:
% MISSING CITATION: Eddington, A. S. 1913, MNRAS 73, 359, "On a formula for correcting statistics for the effects of a known error of observation"
because $w_{\rm pop}$ rises steeply with $z$, a galaxy observed at $z_g$ is
more probably a higher-redshift galaxy scattered down than the reverse, and
the bare kernel under-estimates every host redshift by
$\delta z_{\rm Edd} = \sigma_z^2 s$. The GW data fix
$d_L^\star \propto f(z_t)/h$ with $f(z) \equiv (1+z)I(z)$ and $z_t$ the true
host redshift; matching the model distance at the assumed redshift
$\hat z \simeq z_g$ instead of $z_t \simeq z_g + \sigma_z^2 s$ biases the
peak by
\begin{equation}
  \Delta h \;\simeq\; -\,h\,
  \left.\frac{\mathrm{d}\ln f}{\mathrm{d}z}\right|_{\bar z}
  \sigma_z^2\, s(\bar z)
  \;\equiv\; -\,C(\bar z)\,\sigma_z^2 ,
  \qquad
  \frac{\mathrm{d}\ln f}{\mathrm{d}z} = \frac{1}{1+z} + \frac{1}{IE},
  \label{eq:app:dhlaw}
\end{equation}
% src: docs/derivations/G2b_host_z_volume_prior.md Eq. (2.3)
evaluated at a representative detected-host redshift $\bar z$ ($C$ must
properly be averaged over the detected population; it is convex, growing as
$z^{-2}$ towards low $z$, so the average exceeds $C(z_{\rm med})$). The bias
is negative --- $H_0$ low --- and strictly $\propto \sigma_z^2$ at leading
order.

\paragraph*{Comparison with the measured biases.}
Table~\ref{tab:app:eddz} lists the flat-prior maximum-a-posteriori bias of
the bare-Gaussian kernel measured on the single-host synthetic suite (flat
$\Lambda$CDM, $\Omega_{\rm m} = 0.3$, $h_{\rm true} = 0.72$; this suite,
unlike the main campaign, injects $h = 0.72$). The estimator carries a
$\sigma_z$-independent residual floor of $\approx -0.002$; after subtracting
it, the biases scale as $1 : 4.8 : 10.0$ against
$\sigma_z^2 = 1 : 5.44 : 11.1$ --- the quadratic law holds to
$\sim$10 per cent --- and the implied coefficient
$C_{\rm meas} = -\Delta h/\sigma_z^2 \approx 17$--$20$ is constant to
$\pm 8$ per cent across a factor 11 in $\sigma_z^2$.
% src: docs/derivations/G2b_host_z_volume_prior.md §2.3
Evaluating Eq.~\eqref{eq:app:dhlaw} at $\Omega_{\rm m}=0.3$ gives
$C = 20.1,\ 18.4,\ 13.4,\ 8.9$ at $\bar z = 0.25,\ 0.26,\ 0.30,\ 0.357$
% src: docs/derivations/G2b_host_z_volume_prior.md §2.3 table (C(zbar) column)
(the last being the redshift of the suite's median detected distance), so
$C_{\rm meas}$ corresponds to $\bar z_{\rm eff} \approx 0.25$--$0.27$ ---
inside the detected distribution and, as the convexity of $C(z)$ requires,
below the median $0.30$--$0.36$. Taken at the median literally, the
leading-order amplitude agrees within $\sim$30--50 per cent; allowing the
population average over the realistic detected spread
$z \approx 0.15$--$0.45$, it agrees within its own accuracy.

\paragraph*{Why the exact deconvolution, not the leading-order shift.}
For a GLADE+-like host at $z_g = 0.05$, Eq.~\eqref{eq:app:eddz} gives
$s = 38.1$ and $C(0.05) = 569$; the expansion parameter is
$\sigma_z s = 0.19,\ 0.57,\ 1.33,\ 1.91$ at
$\sigma_z = 0.005,\ 0.015,\ 0.035,\ 0.050$, so perturbation theory is valid
only for $\sigma_z \lesssim 0.015$.
% src: docs/derivations/G2b_host_z_volume_prior.md §2.3 (z_g = 0.05 case)
Exact evaluation of the posterior-mean shift of Eq.~\eqref{eq:app:pgz} at
$z_g = 0.05$ yields $\delta z = +0.0325$ and $+0.0535$ at
$\sigma_z = 0.035$ and $0.050$, against the leading-order values $+0.0467$
and $+0.0953$:
% src: docs/derivations/G2b_host_z_volume_prior.md §2.3 exact-vs-leading table
the shift saturates below the quadratic law but remains comparable to $z_g$
itself, a $\gtrsim 60$ per cent fractional redshift error per host. The
implementation therefore performs the exact quadrature deconvolution of
Eq.~\eqref{eq:app:pgz} rather than the shift
$z_g \to z_g + \sigma_z^2 s$. Two subleading asymmetries are accepted: the
prior is evaluated (not zeroed) where the GW numerator window exceeds the
normalization window, an impropriety bounded by the $6.3\times10^{-5}$
Gaussian $>\!4\sigma$ tail;
% src: docs/derivations/G2b_host_z_volume_prior.md §3.3 item 2
and the analogous population weight in the host-\emph{mass} channel is
treated separately in Appendix~\ref{sec:app:eddm}. The mapping of this
kernel onto the \citet{Gray:2019ksv} in-catalogue likelihood is given in
Appendix~\ref{sec:app:gray}; its effect on posterior calibration is
quantified in Section~\ref{sec:coverage}.
