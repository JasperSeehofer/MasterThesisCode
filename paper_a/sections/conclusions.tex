We have traced the complete failure-and-repair arc of an independently
implemented galaxy-catalogue dark-siren estimator of the
\citet{Gray:2019ksv} family in a regime its published validations never
covered: host redshifts of photometric quality, $\sigma_z/z \sim 0.7$
% src: .planning/gate/G5a_gwcosmo_inspection.md (sigma_z/z ~ 0.7, EMRI/GLADE+ regime)
in the GLADE+ catalogue \citep{Dalya:2021ewn}.  The railed
Hubble-constant posterior is the superposition of three normalization
defects --- an Eddington--Malmquist prior inconsistency of the bare
photometric host-redshift kernel, a completion-term sky factor
evaluated at the peak of the localization Gaussian instead of its
isotropic $1/(4\pi)$ marginal, and an $h$-dependent tilt of the global
selection sum --- all violating one invariant: every population measure
counted exactly once, identically in each numerator and its selection
normalization (Section~\ref{sec:pitfall}).  Two prior-consistent
repairs cure it (Section~\ref{sec:estimators}): the local
ratio-of-sums and the comoving-volume deconvolution of the host
redshift (and host mass) kernels.  On synthetic universes with known
truth, the bare kernel collapses to near-zero frequentist coverage
through a rigid $-0.024$ bias in $h$ scaling as $\sigma_z^2$, while
the volume kernel is calibrated close to nominal
(Section~\ref{sec:coverage}).
% src: results/commission_20260701/scratch/d2/NOTE_calibration_findings.md (RESULTs 1-2)
On identical real GLADE+ data the pre-fix estimator rails at
$h = 0.86$, the $1/(4\pi)$-only fix flips the rail to $0.60$, and
either repair de-rails the posterior onto an interior peak at the
injected truth $h = 0.73$ (Section~\ref{sec:realdata}).
% src: results/commission_20260701/redteam/crux_results.json (prod); derail_matrix_results.json (prod_global, local_ratio, volume_deconv)
The earlier reading of the same data as photometric information
starvation is thereby refuted: prior-inconsistent estimators convert
photometric scatter into coherent, $N$-amplified bias --- a property of
the estimators, never of the data (Section~\ref{sec:postmortem}).

For catalogue dark sirens at photometric-redshift-dominated depths the
lessons are two, of different scope (Section~\ref{sec:codes}).  The
rail itself is a hazard of independent implementation --- structurally
excluded from \textsc{gwcosmo}, absent from the other published
pipelines --- but the LISA and next-generation-detector era will
multiply independent implementations, and the consistency invariant is
cheap to state and to check.  The bare-kernel Eddington bias, by
contrast, is live in the flagship pipeline by an assumption no code
enforces, and no published pipeline has a known-truth calibration test
at $\sigma_z/z \sim 0.7$ in a catalogue-information-dominated analysis
--- precisely the regime that LISA-era dark sirens paired with
Rubin--LSST- and Euclid-scale photometric catalogues will occupy.
Known-truth coverage tests at full photometric width, of which the
released harness is one example, deserve the same standing as the
spectroscopic mock data challenges that established the method.

The limitations are explicit.  The events are simulated, injected at
$h = 0.73$
% src: master_thesis_code/constants.py (H = 0.73)
and analysed against the real GLADE+ field, so every claim concerns
estimator calibration, never a measurement of nature's expansion rate.
The seed600 sample was selected at the pre-$\mathrm{d}t^2$
signal-to-noise scale --- a self-consistent mock universe, valid for
the estimator comparison, but a population louder and shallower
($z \lesssim 0.11$) than a true threshold-20 EMRI campaign
(Sections~\ref{sec:realdata} and~\ref{sec:budget}).
% src: .planning/gate/G8_inner_product_finding.md (physical SNR >= 200, depth z <= 0.11)
Both railed modes sit on grid endpoints, so their unconstrained maxima
may lie outside $[0.60, 0.86]$.
% src: results/commission_20260701/redteam/derail_matrix_results.json (h_values grid endpoints)
And the post-repair systematic budget
of Section~\ref{sec:budget} is dominated by quoted model-scope
choices, of which only the fixed matter-density fiducial reaches the
per-cent level ($\approx +1.5$--$3$ per cent on $H_0$ if the truth
were the Planck value).
% src: .planning/gate/G7_systematics_budget.md (row 6 + detail table)

Three steps follow.  The full-scale confirmation --- the
volume-deconvolved default over the full seed600 sample and the
complete $h$-grid --- has since completed, confirming the de-rail at
production resolution with an interior $\mathrm{MAP} = 0.745$
(Section~\ref{sec:realdata}).
% src: results/commission_20260701/redteam/combined_posterior_voldeconv_fullgrid.json (cluster eval 5732036/5735964 + combine 5735965)
A multi-seed production campaign at the corrected signal-to-noise
scale will re-measure the campaign-time rows of the budget and supply
the deeper, completion-dominated population that a forecast requires.
On that population, the companion paper (Paper~B) will deliver the
forecast-grade EMRI dark-siren $H_0$ constraint
\citep{Babak:2017tow,LISA:2024hlh} --- with the calibrated estimator,
in exactly the regime where the normalization discipline established
here is the difference between a railed posterior and a measurement.
