\subsection{gwcosmo: the rail cannot arise; the bare kernel is present by assumption}

The rail-producing inconsistencies are structurally excluded from \textsc{gwcosmo}, in both of its generations. The selection denominator is built from literally the same prior object as the event numerator: in v1.0.0 the in-catalogue likelihood and its selection factor are sums over the identical smeared redshift samples with identical weights, and in the current line-of-sight (LOS) architecture the catalogue-informed full-sky redshift prior is fed into the injection re-weighting with its normalization explicitly carried into the numerator --- the ``population measure counted exactly once'' rule of Section~\ref{sec:framework} holds by construction, as the papers state the cancellations explicitly. % src: .planning/gate/G5a_gwcosmo_inspection.md (secs 2.2, 6: P1a "structurally excluded")
The sky bookkeeping of Defect~2 likewise cannot arise: every sky quantity is expressed as a per-pixel probability (a fraction of $4\pi$), with the uniform per-pixel prior either cancelling explicitly or applied as an explicit equal-area average, so per-steradian densities are never mixed with per-sky probabilities. % src: .planning/gate/G5a_gwcosmo_inspection.md (sec 3, sec 6: P2 "cannot arise")
As for Defect~3, the O3-era release is literally the local ratio-of-sums of \citet{Gray:2019ksv} (their Eqs.~A9/A10) --- one of the two cures of Section~\ref{sec:estimators} --- while the current generation uses coarse-map effective galaxy counts inside a globally normalized likelihood whose denominator carries the same catalogue-informed prior. % src: .planning/gate/G5a_gwcosmo_inspection.md (sec 4)
Instructively, the \textsc{gwcosmo} authors document an unrelated pathology of \emph{local} normalization (small-number statistics of fine-pixel galaxy counts) and engineer around it \citep{MISSING:Gray2023-los-zprior}; in-catalogue normalization is a recognized, live design point in the field, not an implementation triviality. % src: .planning/gate/G5a_gwcosmo_inspection.md (sec 4, Gray 2023 sec 2.1.4)

What \textsc{gwcosmo} shares with our pre-fix numerator is the bare kernel itself: catalogued host redshifts are marginalized against the truncated catalogue Gaussian with no population measure, in both generations. % src: .planning/gate/G5a_gwcosmo_inspection.md (sec 2.1)
The published defence is definitional: the catalogue redshift distributions are declared to be \emph{posteriors} on the true redshift, in which case no further prior may be applied and the Eddington-type bias of Section~\ref{sec:pitfall} is, by construction, the catalogue provider's problem. % src: .planning/gate/G5a_gwcosmo_inspection.md (sec 2.1, Gray 2023 footnote 10)
This is an assumption of the derivation, not a property enforced or tested by the code, and it is untestable from the catalogue alone; the bias law measured in Section~\ref{sec:coverage} applies to this structure if and only if the catalogue widths are likelihood widths rather than genuine posterior widths. % src: .planning/gate/G5a_gwcosmo_inspection.md (sec 2.1, sec 6: P1b)
The regime question is therefore decisive, and the validation record is thin exactly where it matters. The mock data challenge that established the method used galaxies with no redshift uncertainties at all --- known-truth recovery was validated only at $\sigma_z = 0$ \citep{Gray:2019ksv}; % src: .planning/gate/G5a_gwcosmo_inspection.md (sec 5 item 1)
the subsequent methodological releases were validated by code-vs-code agreement on real GWTC-3 data, which cannot detect a common-mode bias \citep{MISSING:Gray2022-pixelated, MISSING:Gray2023-los-zprior}. % src: .planning/gate/G5a_gwcosmo_inspection.md (sec 5 items 2-3)
Meanwhile the GLADE+ preparation shipped with the code assigns $\sigma_z = 1.5\times10^{-4}$ (spectroscopic), $1.5\times10^{-2}$ (photometric), $0.04\,(1+z)$ (WISE), and $0.36\,z$ (HyperLEDA), so redshifts with $\sigma_z/z$ up to $\sim 0.36$ --- formally $\mathcal{O}(1)$ for photometric entries at $z \lesssim 0.015$ --- pass through the production machinery; passing through is not validation. % src: .planning/gate/G5a_gwcosmo_inspection.md (sec 5, create_glade+.py:60-95)
The closest published approach to our regime, an experiment inflating the Gaussian widths by factors of 2 and 5 (reaching absolute $\sigma_z \approx 0.1$--$0.25$, i.e.\ $\sigma_z/z \gtrsim 0.5$--$1$), was performed on real data: it demonstrates that the posterior is pushed towards the empty-catalogue case --- information wash-out --- but, with no injected truth available, it cannot measure a bias \citep{MISSING:Turski2023-photoz}. % MISSING CITATION: Turski et al. 2023, "Impact of modelling galaxy redshift uncertainties on the gravitational-wave dark standard siren measurement of the Hubble constant", MNRAS, arXiv:2302.12037
% src: .planning/gate/G5a_gwcosmo_inspection.md (sec 5 item 4)
We conclude that no published \textsc{gwcosmo} test validates unbiased $H_0$ recovery in a catalogue-information-dominated analysis at photometric widths --- a frontier its own authors flag as important future territory. % src: .planning/gate/G5a_gwcosmo_inspection.md (sec 5 Answer to Q4, sec 6)

\subsection{CHIMERA, icarogw, and DarkSirensStat: the volume-weighted kernel is published practice}

The other three pipelines all implement the volume-weighted per-galaxy posterior --- the same construction as the deconvolved kernel of Section~\ref{sec:estimators}. \textsc{chimera} multiplies each galaxy's Gaussian by $\mathrm{d}V_c/\mathrm{d}z$ and renormalizes per galaxy, motivating the prior as ``naturally chosen as uniform in comoving volume'' \citep{MISSING:Borghi2024-chimera, Gair:2022zsa}; a bare-Gaussian variant exists in the code but is not on the default path. % src: .planning/gate/G5b_chimera_icarogw_inspection.md (sec 1.1, paper Eq. 12)
\textsc{icarogw}'s single kernel entry point computes, verbatim, ``the EM likelihood in redshift times a uniform in comoving volume prior''; both default kernel options are volume-weighted, and the bare Gaussian survives only as an explicit opt-in. % src: .planning/gate/G5b_chimera_icarogw_inspection.md (sec 2.1, catalog.py:995-1071)
\textsc{DarkSirensStat} --- the earliest of the three (2021), on the real GLADE catalogue with $\sigma_z = 1.5\times10^{-4}$ (spectroscopic) and $1.5\times10^{-2}$ (photometric) --- multiplies its bounded quantile-parametrized redshift distributions by the comoving-volume Jacobian at a fixed fiducial cosmology and refits \citep{MISSING:Finke2021-darksirensstat}. % src: .planning/gate/G5b_chimera_icarogw_inspection.md (sec 3)
All three also keep their completion terms measure-consistent with the catalogue term: \textsc{chimera} distributes the out-of-catalogue mass isotropically per pixel with the uniform $\Delta\Omega_{\rm pix}/4\pi$ measure --- structurally the same isotropic sky marginal as our Defect-2 repair --- while \textsc{icarogw} and \textsc{DarkSirensStat} resolve completeness per line of sight, which is strictly finer. % src: .planning/gate/G5b_chimera_icarogw_inspection.md (secs 1.2, 2.2, 3, synthesis item ii)
Our pre-fix configuration was therefore \emph{outside} published practice, and the repairs of Section~\ref{sec:estimators} bring it into line with it --- independent corroboration that the rail is a curable normalization artifact rather than a property of photometric catalogues (Section~\ref{sec:postmortem}). % src: .planning/gate/G5b_chimera_icarogw_inspection.md (sec 4, implication i)

Two further findings sharpen the picture. First, \textsc{chimera}'s published forecast treats the photometric case as a headline regime, not future work: on known-truth mock catalogues with $\sigma_z = 0.001\,(1+z)$ versus $0.05\,(1+z)$, the correctly weighted kernel yields a \emph{broadened but unbiased} $H_0$ --- $\sigma_{H_0}/H_0 \approx 7$ versus $18$ per cent for an O4-like configuration, and a degradation of up to a factor of $\sim 9$ for an O5-like one --- with no railing. % src: .planning/gate/G5b_chimera_icarogw_inspection.md (sec 1.4, paper Eq. 22 + abstract)
This is exactly the phenomenology of Section~\ref{sec:postmortem}: once the normalization is prior-consistent, photometric widths cost information, not calibration. Second, even reviewed pipelines find numerator/denominator consistency delicate: \textsc{icarogw} carries an in-code acknowledged approximation in which the injection (selection) weights assume an empty catalogue --- a member of the same global-versus-local normalization family as Defect~3, although it is a smooth amplitude effect rather than a rail mechanism, because both sides of its ratio carry the same volume prior in $z$. % src: .planning/gate/G5b_chimera_icarogw_inspection.md (sec 2.3, rates.py:336/351)

\subsection{The unvalidated regime}

The synthesis is a field-level statement in two precise halves. First, the rail itself is a hazard of independent \emph{implementation}, not of the estimator family as published: it is structurally excluded from \textsc{gwcosmo} and absent from the other three codes, and we found it only because we built the estimator from the published equations independently. Since the LISA/ET era will multiply independent implementations, the value of the consistency invariant of Section~\ref{sec:framework} --- the population measure counted exactly once, sky measures commensurate across channels, and the identical kernel in each numerator and its selection factor --- is that it is cheap to state and to check, with \textsc{gwcosmo} as the positive example. % src: .planning/gate/G5a_gwcosmo_inspection.md (sec 6, "Implication for Paper A")
Second, the bare-kernel Eddington bias is not hypothetical field-wide: the flagship pipeline applies the identical bare-Gaussian choice, guarded by a posterior declaration that no code enforces and no catalogue-only test can falsify, and no published known-truth validation of any pipeline exists at $\sigma_z/z \sim 0.7$ in a catalogue-information-dominated analysis --- precisely the regime that LISA-era dark sirens paired with LSST- and Euclid-type photometric catalogues will occupy. % src: .planning/gate/G5a_gwcosmo_inspection.md (secs 5-6); .planning/gate/G5b_chimera_icarogw_inspection.md (secs 1.4, 2.4)
The honest escape routes are complementary: empirically calibrated redshift error models built against overlapping spectroscopic samples \citep{MISSING:Turski2023-photoz}, which resolve the posterior-versus-likelihood ambiguity with data, are equivalent in intent to the volume deconvolution of Section~\ref{sec:estimators}; and known-truth coverage tests at photometric widths, of which Section~\ref{sec:coverage} supplies an example for the estimator family considered here, close the validation gap that assumption alone cannot. % src: .planning/gate/G5a_gwcosmo_inspection.md (secs 2.1, 6)
