The soundness audit behind Sections~\ref{sec:pitfall}--\ref{sec:realdata} produced, beyond the normalization repairs themselves, an inventory of every systematic effect known to act on the recovered $h$. Table~\ref{tab:budget} condenses it. Each row carries one of three dispositions. \textit{Fixed} rows are estimator-calibration defects: each was repaired under a physics-change protocol and is pinned by a regression test. % src: .planning/gate/GATE_SIGNOFF.md (verdict paragraph)
\textit{Quoted} rows are deliberate model-scope choices whose effect on $h$ we state rather than absorb --- standard forecast practice. \textit{Campaign} rows are bounded here and re-measured during the multi-seed production campaign that feeds Paper~B. % src: .planning/gate/G7_systematics_budget.md (preamble + "Reading of the table")

Two entries in the fixed block deserve comment beyond the table. The largest, the missing $\mathrm{d}t^{2}$ normalization that converts the raw discrete Fourier transform to its continuum estimate in the noise-weighted inner product \citep{Babak:2023lro}, does not bias the estimator at all --- it rescales the simulated universe. Pre-fix, every code-level signal-to-noise ratio was the physical one divided by ten, so the nominal detection threshold of 20 selected a physical-SNR~$\geq 200$ population of depth $z \leq 0.11$, with Fisher uncertainties ten times too pessimistic for such loud events. % src: .planning/gate/G8_inner_product_finding.md
Because the identical inner product enters both the event selection and the injection campaign that defines $p_{\rm det}$, the pre-fix datasets remain self-consistent mock universes and every calibration claim in this paper survives unchanged (Section~\ref{sec:realdata}); what the fix changes is the population a given threshold selects, and hence the forecast --- which is why forecast-grade constraints are deferred to Paper~B. % src: .planning/gate/GATE_SIGNOFF.md (open items: "scientifically valid for the de-rail claim")
Second, the Eddington-in-$M$ repair of the optional mass channel (Appendix~\ref{sec:app:eddm}) leaves the one-dimensional posterior essentially untouched while moving the two-dimensional mean from $0.790$ to $0.770$, toward the injected $0.73$; most of that shift is the suppression of spurious edge mass ($0.216 \to 0.023$) rather than a displacement of the interior peak, whose MAP is unchanged on the diagnostic grid. % src: .planning/gate/G7row9_eddington_m_impact.json

Among the quoted rows, only the matter-density fiducial reaches the per-cent level. The pipeline fixes $\Omega_{\mathrm{m}} = 0.2726$, the value matched to the MBH-population model \citep{Babak:2017tow,MISSING:Barausse2012-mbh-population} from which the events are drawn, so the estimator is exactly self-consistent at the injected truth. % MISSING CITATION: Barausse 2012, "The evolution of massive black holes and their spins in their galactic hosts", arXiv:1201.5888
% src: .planning/gate/GATE_SIGNOFF.md (G11)
If the true matter density were instead the Planck value $0.3153$ \citep{Planck:2018vyg}, the $h'$ solving $d_L(z; h', 0.2726) = d_L(z; 0.73, 0.3153)$ rises from $+0.3$ per cent above the truth at $z \approx 0.1$ to $+3.3$ per cent at $z \approx 1.5$, a population-weighted $\approx +1.5$--$3$ per cent on $H_0$ at the post-$\mathrm{d}t^{2}$ population depth. % src: .planning/gate/G7_systematics_budget.md (row 6 + detail table); .planning/gate/GATE_SIGNOFF.md (G11)
The remaining quoted rows affect event rates and population shape rather than calibration --- the P--P tests of Section~\ref{sec:coverage} close at the injected truth --- and the incompleteness of GLADE+ \citep{Dalya:2021ewn} beyond $z \sim 0.3$ is a property of any catalogue-based forecast at LISA depths, to be reported as an in-/out-of-catalogue weight split rather than corrected. % src: .planning/gate/G7_systematics_budget.md (rows 15, 16)

Of the campaign rows, the reference-frame correction is already complete: rebuilding the reduced catalogue with CMB-frame redshifts shifts 99.9 per cent of the rows (median $|\Delta z| = 6\times10^{-4}$), for a measured net effect of $\approx +0.15$ per cent on $H_0$; the committed baselines of Section~\ref{sec:realdata} predate the rebuild and therefore carry this offset, negligible against the $\Delta\mathrm{MAP} \sim 0.1$ normalization effects at issue there. % src: .planning/gate/G7_systematics_budget.md (row 7); .planning/gate/GATE_SIGNOFF.md (z_cmb row); .planning/gate/G9_timeout_scan.md (de-rail scale)
The timeout row is the one campaign item not provably negligible from existing logs. The injection campaign that estimates $p_{\rm det}$ grants each waveform a 30\,s generation budget where the event simulation grants 90\,s, so an event whose waveform takes between 30 and 90\,s survives in the event sample but is dropped from the selection-function estimate --- an unmodelled selection in the likelihood of \citet{Gray:2019ksv} that biases $p_{\rm det}$ low precisely in the slow-waveform region. % src: .planning/gate/G9_timeout_scan.md (D2 budget asymmetry)
Measured rates are 0.59--1.17 per cent of attempted waveforms at the simulation SNR stage, a strikingly stable 1.25 per cent at the injection stage, and $\leq 0.06$ per cent at the Cram\'er--Rao stage; the first-order residual after numerator--denominator cancellation is bounded by the budget-asymmetric sliver, sub-per-cent of the detected sample, but a possible correlation with slowly evolving low-mass systems can only be resolved by the per-parameter timeout logging now installed at every catch site. % src: .planning/gate/G9_timeout_scan.md (§4, §5, §6)

The overall reading of Table~\ref{tab:budget} is the central point of this paper in budget form: every mechanism capable of railing or coherently tilting the posterior sits in the fixed block, and after those repairs the remaining budget is dominated by stated model-scope choices at or below the few-per-cent level --- one to two orders of magnitude below the $\Delta\mathrm{MAP} \sim 0.1$ normalization artifacts that Sections~\ref{sec:pitfall}--\ref{sec:realdata} diagnose and cure. % src: .planning/gate/G7_systematics_budget.md ("Reading of the table"); .planning/gate/G9_timeout_scan.md (de-rail scale)
