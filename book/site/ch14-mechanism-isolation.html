2. The question this chapter asks

A confirmed defect is not yet a repair. The /physics-change protocol this book has cited since Chapter 1 (see .claude/rules/physics-validation.md) demands, before a single line of production code changes: the old formula, the new formula, a reference or derivation, dimensional analysis, and a limiting-case check. The venue-transfer campaign supplied the first item and nothing else — it confirmed that the estimator is wrong, not which term is wrong. Opening the gate with only the old formula written is not opening it at all. The mechanism-isolation study that follows exists to answer one question: which term of the estimator produces the collapse?

3. Six candidates, four closed before a single instrument run

The register that opened the study named six mechanisms. Four of them were refuted by L0 — analysis, toy models, and code reads against the estimator’s own source — before a single dosed seed was run on the real instrument. This is the methodological heart of the chapter: cheap, decisive reasoning did most of the killing.

M3 REFUTED h-dependent truncation of the unrenormalised z-kernel — the mechanism is real (confirmed at the code level) but implies a MAP displacement of +6.0×10−7 against the observed +3.72×10−2 — a factor 6.2×104 short, and the implied shift decreases with dose, the wrong trend. route: amplitude and dose-trend
M4 REFUTED α(h) is σz-blind — the term M4 says is missing is identically 1 at every σz (exactness argument). Deleting α(h) entirely from 2,400 stored posteriors leaves a σz-keyed bias of +0.0165 — the keying survives total deletion of the term M4 accuses. route: the missing term is 1
M1 REFUTED (sole mechanism) missing comoving-volume / rate prior — predicts H0 biased LOW by ~0.02–0.04 (λ > 0 throughout the venue’s population), the opposite sign of the observed defect. Retained only as a compounding negative quadratic term. route: sign
M5 REFUTED AS STATED equal-weight 1/K candidate prior over a smeared population — the prior half is exonerated (reweighting never attenuates the bias); with zero population scatter, 76% of the bias survives. A modified carrier M5′ (the estimator’s own over-broad effective candidate measure) survives all three constraints instead. route: attribution
M2 CLOSED — REFUTED missing Jacobian in the point-distance term — would bias the T-0 anchor. T-0 is clean: 200/200 seeds argmax exactly on truth, zero rails. route: the anchor
M5′ SURVIVES, CONFIRMED ON THE INSTRUMENT the carrier that remains: the estimator’s own over-broad effective candidate measure, times the marginalisation Jacobian. Positive sign, linear in σz, vanishing at σz=0 — not repairable by any candidate reweighting. Confirmed by the split-dose experiment (§6), superseded in scope by the 2-D dose surface’s gate×amplifier finding (§7). route: survives every registered constraint

Four candidates (M1, M2, M3, M4) closed at L0, at a cost the study itself puts at a few CPU-minutes for M3 alone. Only M5/M5′ needed the instrument.

4. The parity argument

Before any split-dose or 2-D scan, one piece of reasoning did more work than any single measurement: an argument from the algebraic structure of a Gaussian convolution, sharp enough to kill an entire class of candidate mechanism at once, not just one member of it.

Gaussian convolution is exp(σ²∂²/2), an expansion in even powers of σ only. Therefore every “we convolved wrong” story — the whole kernel-mismatch class — is O(σz²) at leading order and predicts Rdose ∝ σz: a 3.5× change across the B1→B2 dose lever. A ratio of 0.92, near unity and nowhere near the predicted 3.5×, kills every symmetric-kernel-mismatch story simultaneously — whatever carries the defect must act somewhere the parity argument does not reach: at the integrand peak, not in a mis-shaped tail. The M3 corollary makes the same point independently: because the estimator’s truncation window is pinned at ±4σ in the GW-likelihood variable, anything confined to the wings is capped at ~10−4 per event. A surviving mechanism must act at the integrand peak.

5. Cheap experiments beat expensive ones

The venue-transfer campaign that confirmed the defect ran the decision cell at N = 400 seeds. It did not need to be that large to be decisive, and the mechanism study did not repeat that scale: the per-seed effect is ~7σ on its own (bias +0.037 against a per-seed MAP spread of ~0.005), so mechanism cells needed only N ~ 15–25 to separate classifications by ≫10σ.

6. The split-dose result, and its inversion

Before the 2-D scan, one decisive arm was registered: dose the host redshift only (E1-host) or the impostor sea only (E1-imp), holding the other exact, and see which one carries the bias. The registered prediction named the impostor sea as the carrier — predicted ≥0.030 — and the host as inert — predicted ≤0.012.

The measurement runs the opposite way from the registered guess: the impostor sea alone reproduces almost nothing (consistent with zero), while the host alone carries a small but nonzero +0.004. Worse for either reading: neither arm, nor their sum, reproduces N-0 — 0.004 + 0.000 = 0.004 against a null-arm bias of 0.035. The system is strongly non-additive: whatever the two doses do together, it is not what they do apart.

Gate × amplifier FINDING SCAN_READOUT.md; score_2d_scan_output.json; ledger row #101 The host is an absolute gate: the fhost=0 row is exactly +0.000000 at every impostor dose, 60/60 seeds, a degenerate posterior. The impostor sea is a graded amplifier: removing it (the fimp=0 column) leaves +0.0047…+0.0060 across the dosed host levels — roughly 15% of the fully-dosed S33 effect. The impostor sea carries the remaining ~85%, but only once the host gate is open. Neither ingredient alone is close to sufficient; together they are the whole defect.

The registered decision tree fires BRANCH 2 — INTERACTION-BILINEAR: non-additivity at S33 (D = +0.033667, 23.4σ) and a shape-interaction statistic at S23 (b = +0.023650, 28.2σ above its own registered boundary). But the branch’s meaning — a genuine strictly-bilinear product form, D = I·fhost·fimp — is refuted by the scan’s own statistics at ≥10σ: b(S23) sits +10.33σ above H-INT’s own point prediction (+14.64σ realized); the alternative threshold hypothesis, H-THRESH, is independently refuted at 17.96σ/50.18σ. The branch fires, but its pre-stated meaning may not be quoted. The surface is a gate and an amplifier, not a product of two independent terms — and no functional form for it has been fitted.

8. Two pre-registration defects, recorded not repaired

Honest bookkeeping about the instruments themselves, not just the physics: the mechanism study surfaced two design faults in its own pre-registered decision statistics. Per the project’s anti-tuning discipline, neither threshold was moved after the fact — both are recorded as defects for a future amendment.

It was settled on data, at N = 100 (MN0X mean bias +0.037250 ± 0.000494, |Δ| = 0.000013, 153.8× inside the unchanged window) — rather than the window being widened on principle. 9. What is still unknown

The mechanism study closes with a description, not a formula. Every candidate named at registration is closed, refuted-as-stated, or reduced to a compounding secondary term; the surviving structure — a host gate multiplying a graded impostor-sea amplifier — is characterised at high significance and has no admissible functional form fitted to it. Both registered shapes for it are refuted. No third shape has been proposed.

Stated plainly: the new-formula slot is empty. Filling it is a derivation — an author-gated step this readout does not take. PHYSICS_CHANGE_INTAKE_DOSSIER.md §2 / §3

The physics-change intake package this arc opened fills the one gate slot it can fill today — the old formula, exactly, with per-symbol provenance — and states sixteen constraints (C1–C16) any future candidate term must satisfy: it must vanish identically when the host is exact at any impostor dose; it must be non-separable in the two doses, since the impostor response’s shape itself changes with the host dose; it must act at the integrand peak, not in the truncated wings the parity argument already excludes; and it must survive the α-deletion test that M4 already ran. No published equation is known to hold in this regime — the literature register carries a standing warning that the reduction this estimator relies on assumes perfect galaxy redshifts (Gair et al. 2023, arXiv:2212.08694 §2.3), a condition this project’s own photo-z errors violate at every venue. There is nothing to derive to, yet. The chapter ends here, not because the story is finished, but because this is where the measured record currently ends.

The branch-2/branch-5 question, still open The registered decision tree’s branch-2 condition fires, but branch-5’s condition (“a resolved but non-bilinear and non-threshold surface”) is also factually satisfied and was, at the time of the readout, unreachable in the registered ordering. Which reading governs — and whether branch 2’s listed consequence (any candidate term must vanish when either dose is removed) is retained even though its meaning clause is barred — is a standing author ruling this chapter does not resolve, per SCAN_READOUT.md §9 item 1.
