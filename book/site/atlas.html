The Atlas

The whole measurement on one map — click any node to open its formula; click inside a formula to go deeper.

Two pipelines make this project: a GPU simulation pipeline that turns a synthetic EMRI population into per-event measurement forecasts, and a CPU inference pipeline that turns those forecasts plus a galaxy catalogue into a posterior on the Hubble constant. They meet in a single CSV file. Every node below is a formula you can open; the scar-tissue toggle overlays the project's own history — every [PHYSICS]-gated fix, ratification and falsified idea, on the exact term it touched.

How to read this page

Solid arrows are the production data flow; dashed arrows are shared physics feeding both pipelines; the dotted arrow is validation watching over the result. Inside a formula, highlighted terms respond to hover (what is this?) and click (go deeper, where a term is itself a mapped formula). The history timelines are not decoration: every entry is a real commit that passed the physics-change gate, and the falsified/exonerated entries are ideas that were honestly tried and honestly retired — the same evidence discipline as the museum.
