Part A — the candidate: who owns M-2’s matched 2D overlap residual?

A.1 What M-2 measured

During the Hitchhiker cross-term thread (Chapter 12, timeline entries of 2026-08-05 onward), a cheap stratified read — M-2 — asked whether the 385 events involved in sky-and-distance overlap pairs (the C-4 census) behave differently from the 1203 non-overlap controls. The statistic is the per-event chord ln L(0.60) − ln L(0.73) on the combined likelihood columns of event_likelihoods.csv; positive means the event prefers low h. Two reads were run, and the difference between them is half the story.

Adjudicated finding FINDING m2_results.json; independent recompute m2_adjudicate.py, CONFIRMED After 1-NN matching on the overlap events’ selection drivers (log₁₀ ball-radius chord, SNR), a residual +0.02225 (idealized/iiib) / +0.02070 (joint r1) nats/event survives in the 2D channel at both venues — matched sign-flip p = 1.5e-04 / 1.0e-04, and it survives the cluster-robust secondary test (p = 0.0050 / 0.0042) that corrects for control re-use (234 unique controls serving 385 pairs). The overlap stratum pulls toward low h. The 1D channel does not show it: NULL at iiib (p = 0.098) and marginal-but-fragile at joint r1 (p = 0.0414 primary, cluster-robust p = 0.137 fails). An independent adjudication (own implementations, seed 777777, 40k permutations) reproduced every quoted digit; discrepancies were Monte-Carlo-level only.

A.2 The trap that was almost walked into — shown, not asserted

The unmatched read is roughly twice as large: overlap-minus-control differences of +0.0436 to +0.0498 nats/event, permutation p at the floor (5.0e-05) in every venue–channel cell. Taken at face value that number is mostly selection: overlap events are, by construction, the events with big localisation balls in dense sky — before matching, the strata differ by a standardized mean difference of 1.227 in log₁₀ ball radius and −0.381 in SNR. Matching collapses those imbalances to 0.0027 and 0.0152 and takes the 1D “effect” with them — the 1D conclusion flips from unmatched to matched. This is exactly the failure mode amendment [A2] of the research cycle exists to catch (verbatim: “any Σ-over-events statistic compared across venues/configs/eras requires a paired/stratified per-event read alongside the aggregate”). The 2D residual is what is left after the trap is discharged.

2D channel, overlap-minus-control chord difference in nats/event (positive = overlap stratum prefers low h). Faded bars: the unmatched read — ≈2.2× larger, and mostly selection (the [A2] trap). Solid bars: the matched residual — the finding. Source: crossterm_instrument/m2_results.json, confirmed by m2_adjudicate.py.

Scale context, stated honestly in the read itself: +0.021 nats/event over 385 events is ~8 nats aggregate if coherent — small against the hundreds-of-nats class sums this book has handled, but real, one-directional, and present at both venues, including the idealized venue with no photo-z scatter. One caveat carries over verbatim: the matched control assignment is identical across venues (the covariates come from the shared CRB CSV), so the two venues replicate the likelihoods, not the matching.

A.3 The obvious suspect is excluded

The natural first owner was the mechanism the whole thread was built around: the Eq. (31) pairwise likelihood-factorization cross-term (Gair et al., arXiv:2212.08694) — overlap events are precisely the events that share candidate galaxies. That hypothesis was measured, not argued, and it is dead for this purpose:

Adjudicated finding RATIFIED NEGLECT_TRIGGER_REGISTER.md; author ruling 2026-08-07, ledger §5 The class-summed, mixture-composed cross-term chord is T = 5.65e-05 (joint r1, 2D) / 5.38e-06 (iiib, 2D) nats against a locked negligibility band X = 2.78 — a minimum margin of 4.92e+04×, adjudicated bit-identical by an independent recompute. And the composed 2D chord has the opposite sign to the M-2 residual. NEGLECT-WITH-NUMBER was accepted by the author in all four venue × channel cells, conditioned on a six-trigger re-evaluation register. Whatever owns the matched residual, it is not the Eq. (31) cross-term.

A.4 The live clue: coherence exists, and the composition annihilates it

The exclusion measurement did more than exclude — it localized where the residual’s sign lives. The raw catalogue-leg 2D cross-term chords are positive (low-h-preferring) at both venues: +2.507 nats class-summed at joint r1 (+0.0116 at iiib). The coherence M-2 sees is physically present in the catalogue leg. But the posterior never consumes the raw leg: each event’s catalogue contribution enters through the mixture with per-event share r = w_G·L_cat/combined ≈ 3e-04–1.6e-03, so per-pair terms are crushed by a composition factor F = r_i·r_j with median ~1.4e-07–2.5e-06 — five orders of magnitude — and the h-dependence of w_G itself (falling 0.0957 → 0.0556 across the grid, ×1.72) reverses the class-level chord sign. The full annihilation anatomy, with the log-scale chain diagram, is Chapter 12 §4.

That is the constraint any candidate owner must satisfy, and it is sharp: whatever moves the matched residual must act outside the annihilated catalogue path, or act through the composition weights themselves. A mechanism living purely inside the raw catalogue-leg coupling cannot reach the posterior at the measured size — the composition kills it. The routes left standing pass through the completion leg (which is not annihilated), through quantities correlated with overlap-stratum membership (density, clustering), or through the mixture weights w_G / g_frac that decide what survives composition.

The +0.021 was measured on the combined column; this localizes which term carries it — a completion-leg carrier, a catalogue-leg carrier surviving composition, or a weight-driven carrier each leave a different signature across the columns.
    (ii) Residual-confounding check — add catalogue-density / sky-position covariates (ball galaxy count, local catalogue density, sky coordinates) to the matching. Overlap events sit in denser sky by construction; the current matching balances ball radius and SNR but never density itself. If the residual attenuates toward zero under the richer matching, that is evidence for confounding, not mechanism — and finding that out costs minutes. Cost: CPU-minutes, zero new production compute — every input already exists under results/run_20260804_postfix/*/diagnostics/. Exit: the residual localized to component(s), or dissolved into confounding.
  3. Stage 2 — pre-registration, only for what survives. Whatever stage 1 leaves standing gets a pre-registered test with CONFIRM/MIXED/REFUTE bands locked from the stage-1 cheap bounds — the M-2 pattern: the negligibility band is derived from a measured bound before the instrument runs, never invented in advance. If stage 1 dissolves the residual entirely, the cycle stops there and the chronicle records a confounding verdict — that is a success, not a failure. Stages 3–6 (measure, calibration gate, decision, chronicle) follow as the cycle prescribes, if and only if a mechanism survives.

A.6 The hypotheses, framed fairly

Four candidate owners, deliberately not ranked. Each is stated with the observation that motivates it and the stage-1 signature that would implicate or clear it; none is pre-judged. The point of the component decomposition is that it discriminates among all four in one read.

H-a — completion-leg difference The completion leg is the path the composition does not annihilate. If overlap-stratum events’ L_comp (or its mass factor g_frac — ruled correct physics, which does not preclude it correlating with stratum membership) has a systematically different h-slope, the residual lives there. Stage-1 signature: matched residual reproduces on the L_comp / g_frac columns, not on L_cat.
H-b — g_frac–clustering correlation g_frac has 1587 distinct per-event values; overlap events sample denser sky. A correlation between the mass-factor slope and local clustering would make the overlap stratum inherit a different effective g(h) without any defect anywhere. Stage-1 signature: g_frac-column residual present AND attenuating when density covariates enter the matching.
H-c — residual selection confounding The matching balances log ball radius and SNR — two covariates. If density (or another driver of overlap membership) independently shapes the likelihood chord, the +0.021 is a matching artifact, not physics. Stage-1 signature: the combined-column residual shrinks toward zero under density/sky-position matching.
H-d — composition-weight h-dependence The annihilation argument is itself built on w_G(h) and the per-event shares. A stratum-dependent interaction between w_G’s h-fall and the per-event mixture could move overlap events differently — the “through the weights” route the register explicitly flags as attacking the composition. Stage-1 signature: residual visible in the w_G-composed pieces but in neither bare leg.

The governing constraint on the whole hunt is the author’s standing value ruling, quoted because it decides what counts as success:

“our overarching goal is a scientifically sound project with novel insights and not to get rid of the bias by any means — scientific correctness and new insights are valued higher.” Author governance note, 2026-08-05, BIAS_HISTORY_LEDGER.md §5 — binding on all adjudications.

Under that ruling, “the residual is confounding” and “the residual is a real clustering–g_frac correlation” are both acceptable endings — what is not acceptable is declaring an owner without the component-level measurement, or burying an unowned effect because it is small.

Trap — “the overlap events differ by +0.05 nats, so the effect is twice what this chapter says” That is the unmatched read, and it is mostly selection — the [A2] trap in its textbook form. Overlap events have far larger localisation balls (a standardized mean difference of 1.227 in log₁₀ radius); comparing them to unmatched controls measures the selection, not the stratum. The finding of record is the matched +0.021 — and even that number is owed the confounding check of stage 1(ii) before any mechanism claim is built on it.
Trap — “the cross-term was excluded, so likelihood-factorization is safe for good” The NEGLECT is conditional, not absolute: it stands on a six-trigger register, and under a raw-scored convention three of four cells would already exceed the band. Two of Part B’s open decisions (the N-2 adoption, issue #53) are named triggers of that register — deciding them re-opens the cross-term arithmetic by construction, cheaply and by design.

Issue #53 — the 3σ-vs-2σ z-window OPEN

What it is
get_redshift_outer_bounds declares a sigma_multiplier argument and silently ignores it: the body hardcodes 3 × distance_error while the production call site requests 2.0. The candidate-host ball z-window is therefore 3σ, wider than the code asks for — the same silently-ignored-argument defect class as issue #4.
Evidence on file
Verified bit-exactly during the M-4 adjudication: from-scratch ball rebuilds match the production emits only with the hardcoded-3σ window. Ball-geometry consequences are real — the window width decides the candidate-host set, hence the H₀ likelihood itself.
What hangs on it
Which width is intended is an author physics call (/physics-change), not a bug fix. The trigger register records the direction explicitly: a 2σ adoption shrinks the balls and reduces sharing — favorable to the cross-term NEGLECT, does NOT fire trigger (c); any enlargement or localisation-model change does fire it.
Cheapest next step
An author ruling on the intended width, through /physics-change (derivation, limiting case, regression pin). If 2σ: an optional L1 census refresh is bookkeeping, not re-adjudication. No code moves before the ruling.

How Part A and Part B interlock
