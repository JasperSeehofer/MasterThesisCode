3. Gate (vii): the dark-leg tilt is composition, not per-event

Before the frozen-g run, gate (vii) asked a narrower question: does the catalogue leg itself tilt between h=0.73 and h=0.81 in the post-fix runs? A pairwise-survivor check (events with a defined catalogue likelihood at both h-points, in both channels) found the dark-class tilt −604.8 nats in the joint/observed venue (534 survivors) and −241.7 nats in the idealized venue (219 survivors) — while the in-catalogue leg barely moves (+0.13 / −3.8 nats respectively, 73–75 survivors). The paired check confirmed this is composition-dominated: the large numbers come from summing many dark events each shifting a little in the same direction, not from a few outliers. This ruled out a narrow per-event pathology as the driver and pointed at exactly the kind of systematic, class-wide h-slope that g_frac(h) turned out to carry.
